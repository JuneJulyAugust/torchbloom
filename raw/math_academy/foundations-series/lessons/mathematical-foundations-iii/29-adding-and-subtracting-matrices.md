## Lesson

### Introduction

We add two matrices by adding the corresponding entries. We can only do this if they have the same number of rows and columns, that is, they have the same dimensions.

For example, consider the matrices

$$


[\begin{aligned}1 & 3 \\ 2 & 4\end{aligned}]


$$

We see that both $A$ and $B$ are $2\times 2$ matrices, so we can add them. The sum ${A} + {B}\,$ is given by

$$


\begin{aligned}𝐴+𝐵 & =[\begin{matrix}1 & 3 \\ 2 & 4\end{matrix}]+[\begin{matrix}1 & 0 \\ 0 & −1\end{matrix}] \\ & =[\begin{matrix}1+1 & 3+0 \\ 2+0 & 4+(−1)\end{matrix}] \\ & =[\begin{matrix}2 & 3 \\ 2 & 3\end{matrix}].\end{aligned}


$$

In general, the sum of two matrices is another matrix with the same dimensions.

### Example: Adding Square Matrices

#### Question

Calculate $A+B,$ where

$$


[\begin{aligned}1 & −5 \\ −2 & 3\end{aligned}]


$$

#### Explanation

We add the corresponding entries:

$$


\begin{aligned}𝐴+𝐵 & =[\begin{matrix}1 & −5 \\ −2 & 3\end{matrix}]+[\begin{matrix}−10 & 2 \\ 2 & −4\end{matrix}] \\ & =[\begin{matrix}1−10 & −5+2 \\ −2+2 & 3−4\end{matrix}] \\ & =[\begin{matrix}−9 & −3 \\ 0 & −1\end{matrix}]\end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Adding Non-Square Matrices

#### Question

Calculate ${A}+ {B},$ where

$$


[\begin{aligned}1 & 3 & 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 4 & −1 & 2\end{aligned}]


$$

#### Explanation

We add the corresponding entries:

$$


\begin{aligned}𝐴+𝐵 & =[\begin{matrix}1 & 3 & 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 4 & −1 & 2\end{matrix}]+[\begin{matrix}−2 & −3 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & −2 & 3 & 1\end{matrix}] \\ & =[\begin{matrix}1+(−2) & 3+(−3) & 1+1 & 0+1 \\ 2+1 & 4+(−2) & −1+3 & 2+1\end{matrix}] \\ & =[\begin{matrix}−1 & 0 & 2 & 1 \\ 3 & 2 & 2 & 3\end{matrix}]\end{aligned}


$$

### Subtracting Matrices

We can subtract matrices in the same way that we add matrices: we subtract the corresponding entries. Again, we can only do this if they have the same number of rows and columns, that is, they have the same dimensions.

For example, consider the matrices

$$


[\begin{aligned}1 & 3 \\ 2 & 4\end{aligned}]


$$

We see that both $A$ and $B$ are $2\times 2$ matrices, so, we can subtract them. The difference ${A} - {B}\,$ is given by

$$


\begin{aligned}𝐴−𝐵 & =[\begin{matrix}1 & 3 \\ 2 & 4\end{matrix}]−[\begin{matrix}1 & 0 \\ 0 & −1\end{matrix}] \\ & =[\begin{matrix}1−1 & 3−0 \\ 2−0 & 4−(−1)\end{matrix}] \\ & =[\begin{matrix}0 & 3 \\ 2 & 5\end{matrix}].\end{aligned}


$$

Again, the difference of two matrices is another matrix with the same dimensions.

### Example: Subtracting Matrices

#### Question

Calculate $A-B,$ where

$$


\begin{aligned}2 & −1 \\ −1 & 0 \\ 3 & 2\end{aligned}


$$

#### Explanation

We subtract the corresponding entries:

$$


\begin{aligned}𝐴−𝐵 & =\begin{matrix}2 & −1 \\ −1 & 0 \\ 3 & 2\end{matrix}−\begin{matrix}0 & −1 \\ 2 & −1 \\ −1 & 3\end{matrix} \\ & =\begin{matrix}2−0 & −1−(−1) \\ −1−2 & 0−(−1) \\ 3−(−1) & 2−3\end{matrix} \\ & =\begin{matrix}2 & 0 \\ −3 & 1 \\ 4 & −1\end{matrix}\end{aligned}


$$
