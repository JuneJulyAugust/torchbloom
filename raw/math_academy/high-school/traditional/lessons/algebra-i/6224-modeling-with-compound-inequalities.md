### Example: Constructing and Solving Compound Inequalities With Multiple Variables

#### Question

A branding consultant charges a base fee of $b$ dollars and receives a royalty equal to $10\%$ of the product revenue $x$ generated from a marketing campaign. For a particular contract, the consultant wants total compensation to be at least $1.4$ times and at most $1.8$ times the base fee. What inequality shows all possible values of $x$ that meet this goal?

#### Explanation

The consultant earns $10\%$ of the revenue. So, if the total revenue is $x$ dollars, they earn $0.10x$ dollars.

Additionally, there is a base fee of $b$ dollars. Therefore, the total earned, in dollars, is

$$


b + 0.10x.


$$

Since the consultant wants total earnings to be at least $1.4$ times and at most $1.8$ times the base fee, we must have

$$


1.4b \leq b + 0.10x \leq 1.8b.


$$

Subtracting $b$ throughout, we get

$$


\begin{aligned}0.4𝑏≤0.10𝑥 & ≤0.8𝑏.\end{aligned}


$$

Then, we divide by $0.10{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}\frac{0.4𝑏}{0.10}≤𝑥 & ≤\frac{0.8𝑏}{0.10} \\ 4𝑏≤𝑥 & ≤8𝑏\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, the correct inequality is

$$


4b \leq x \leq 8b.


$$
