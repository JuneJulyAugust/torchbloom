## Lesson

### Introduction

The double-angle formula

$$



\sin{2x}=2\sin{x}\cos{x}



$$

can be used to simplify complicated trigonometric expressions. For instance, suppose we want to simplify the expression

$$



\dfrac{\sin{2x}}{2\cos^2{x}}.



$$

Using the double-angle formula, we can simplify the given expression, as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}\frac{sin⁡2𝑥}{2cos^{2}⁡𝑥} & =\frac{2sin⁡𝑥cos⁡𝑥}{2cos⁡𝑥cos⁡𝑥} \\ & =\frac{2cos⁡𝑥⋅sin⁡𝑥}{2cos⁡𝑥⋅cos⁡𝑥} \\ & =\frac{sin⁡𝑥}{cos⁡𝑥} \\ & =tan⁡𝑥\end{aligned}



$$

### Example: Simplifying Expressions Using the Double-Angle Formula for Sine

#### Question

Simplify the expression $\dfrac{\sin^2{x}}{\sin{2x}}.$

#### Explanation

First, we recall the double-angle formula for sine:

$$



\sin{2x} = 2\sin{x}\cos{x}



$$

Substituting the above into the given expression, we get

$$



\begin{aligned}\frac{sin^{2}⁡𝑥}{sin⁡2𝑥} & =\frac{sin⁡𝑥sin⁡𝑥}{2sin⁡𝑥cos⁡𝑥} \\ & =\frac{sin⁡𝑥sin⁡𝑥}{2sin⁡𝑥cos⁡𝑥} \\ & =\frac{sin⁡𝑥}{2cos⁡𝑥} \\ & =\frac{1}{2}tan⁡𝑥.\end{aligned}



$$

### Example: Simplifying Expressions Containing the Cosecant of a Double Angle

#### Question

Simplify the expression $2\tan x \csc 2x.$

#### Explanation

First, recall that

$$



\tan x = \dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}, \qquad \csc 2x = \dfrac{1}{\color{blue}\sin 2x}.



$$

Now, using the double-angle formula for sine,

$$



\sin{2x} = 2\sin{x}\cos{x},



$$

we simplify the expression as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}2tan⁡𝑥csc⁡2𝑥 & =2⋅\frac{sin⁡𝑥}{cos⁡𝑥}⋅\frac{1}{sin⁡2𝑥} \\ & =\frac{2sin⁡𝑥}{cos⁡𝑥}⋅\frac{1}{2sin⁡𝑥cos⁡𝑥} \\ & =\frac{2sin⁡𝑥}{cos⁡𝑥}⋅\frac{1}{2sin⁡𝑥cos⁡𝑥} \\ & =\frac{1}{cos^{2}⁡𝑥} \\ & =sec^{2}⁡𝑥\end{aligned}



$$

### Example: Simplifying Advanced Trigonometric Expressions Using the Double-Angle Formula for Sine

#### Question

Write $\cos{x}\csc{x}\sec^2{x}$ as a single trigonometric function.

#### Explanation

First, we simplify the given expression using the following identities:

$$



\csc x = \dfrac{1}{\sin x}, \qquad \sec x = \dfrac{1}{\cos x}



$$

Substituting the above into our expression, we get

$$



\begin{aligned}cos⁡𝑥csc⁡𝑥sec^{2}⁡𝑥 & =cos⁡𝑥⋅\frac{1}{sin⁡𝑥}⋅\frac{1}{cos^{2}⁡𝑥} \\ & =\frac{cos⁡𝑥}{sin⁡𝑥cos^{2}⁡𝑥} \\ & =\frac{cos⁡𝑥}{sin⁡𝑥cos⁡𝑥cos⁡𝑥} \\ & =\frac{cos⁡𝑥}{sin⁡𝑥cos⁡𝑥cos⁡𝑥} \\ & =\frac{1}{sin⁡𝑥cos⁡𝑥}.\end{aligned}



$$

Now, we recall the double-angle formula for sine:

$$



2\sin{x}\cos{x} = \sin{2x}



$$

Substituting the above into our expression, we finally get

$$



\begin{aligned}\frac{1}{sin⁡𝑥cos⁡𝑥} & =\frac{2}{2sin⁡𝑥cos⁡𝑥} \\ & =\frac{2}{sin⁡2𝑥} \\ & =2csc⁡2𝑥.\end{aligned}



$$
