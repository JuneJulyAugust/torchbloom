## Lesson

### Introduction

Let's recall the Pythagorean trigonometric identity:

$$



\cos^2 \theta + \sin^2 \theta = 1



$$

Dividing both sides by $\cos^2 \theta,$ we obtain our secant-tangent identity, as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}\frac{cos^{2}⁡𝜃+sin^{2}⁡𝜃}{cos^{2}⁡𝜃} & =\frac{1}{cos^{2}⁡𝜃} \\ \frac{cos^{2}⁡𝜃}{cos^{2}⁡𝜃}+\frac{sin^{2}⁡𝜃}{cos^{2}⁡𝜃} & =\frac{1}{cos^{2}⁡𝜃} \\ 1+(\frac{sin⁡𝜃}{cos⁡𝜃})^{2} & =(\frac{1}{cos⁡𝜃})^{2} \\ 1+tan^{2}⁡𝜃 & =sec^{2}⁡𝜃\end{aligned}



$$

If we subtract $1$ from both sides, we isolate the $\tan^2\theta$ term.

$$



\tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta - 1



$$

This is an **alternate form** of the secant-tangent identity.

We can get a second alternate form if we again start with

$$



1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta.



$$

Subtracting $\tan^2\theta$ from both sides, we get

$$



\sec^2\theta - \tan^2\theta = 1.



$$

We can use these alternate forms to simplify trigonometric expressions. Let's see an example.

### Example: Simplifying an Expression Using the Alternate Forms of the Secant-Tangent Identity

#### Question

Simplify $\dfrac{1}{\sec^2 \theta - \tan^2 \theta}.$

#### Explanation

First, we recall the different forms of the secant-tangent Identity:

$$



\begin{aligned} & (1) & 1+tan^{2}⁡𝜃=sec^{2}⁡𝜃 \\ & (2) & tan^{2}⁡𝜃=sec^{2}⁡𝜃−1 \\ & (3) & sec^{2}⁡𝜃−tan^{2}⁡𝜃=1\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, we can rewrite our expression using $(3),$ as follows:

$$



\dfrac{1}{\color{blue}\sec^2 \theta - \tan^2 \theta} = \dfrac{1}{\color{blue} 1} = 1



$$

Therefore, we conclude that

$$



\dfrac{1}{\sec^2 \theta - \tan^2 \theta} = 1.



$$

### Factoring an Alternate Form of the Secant-Tangent Identity

It's helpful to remember that the alternate forms of the secant-tangent identity can be factored as a difference of squares.

For example, let's consider the identity

$$



\tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta - 1.



$$

Factoring the right-hand side as a difference of squares, we have

$$



\tan^2\theta = (\sec\theta - 1)(\sec\theta + 1).



$$

Similarly, if we start with the identity

$$



\sec^2\theta - \tan^2\theta = 1,



$$

we can factor the left-hand side as a difference of squares, as follows:

$$



(\sec\theta - \tan\theta) (\sec\theta + \tan\theta) = 1



$$

### Example: Simplifying an Expression by Factoring Alternative Forms of the Secant-Tangent Identity

#### Question

Simplify $\dfrac{1}{\sec\theta + \tan\theta}.$

#### Explanation

First, we recall the different forms of the secant-tangent Identity:

$$



\begin{aligned} & (1) & 1+tan^{2}⁡𝜃=sec^{2}⁡𝜃 \\ & (2) & tan^{2}⁡𝜃=sec^{2}⁡𝜃−1 \\ & (3) & sec^{2}⁡𝜃−tan^{2}⁡𝜃=1\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, we can rewrite our expression using $(3),$ as follows:

$$



\dfrac{\color{blue}1}{\sec{\theta}+\tan{\theta}} = \dfrac{\color{blue}\sec^2{\theta} - \tan^2{\theta}}{\sec{\theta} + \tan{\theta}}



$$

Next, we can further simplify our expression by factoring the numerator as a difference of squares, as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}\frac{sec^{2}⁡𝜃−tan^{2}⁡𝜃}{sec⁡𝜃+tan⁡𝜃} & =\frac{(sec⁡𝜃−tan⁡𝜃)(sec⁡𝜃+tan⁡𝜃)}{sec⁡𝜃+tan⁡𝜃} \\ & =\frac{(sec⁡𝜃−tan⁡𝜃)(sec⁡𝜃+tan⁡𝜃)}{(sec⁡𝜃+tan⁡𝜃)} \\ & =sec⁡𝜃−tan⁡𝜃\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, we conclude that

$$



\dfrac{1}{\sec\theta + \tan\theta} = \sec{\theta} - \tan{\theta}.



$$

### Example: Simplifying an Expression by Factoring and Using Various Forms of the Secant-Tangent Identity

#### Question

Express $\tan^2 x\sec^2 x - \tan^4 x +1$ in terms of $\sec x$ only.

#### Explanation

First, we recall the different forms of the secant-tangent Identity:

$$



\begin{aligned} & (1) & 1+tan^{2}⁡𝑥=sec^{2}⁡𝑥 \\ & (2) & tan^{2}⁡𝑥=sec^{2}⁡𝑥−1 \\ & (3) & sec^{2}⁡𝑥−tan^{2}⁡𝑥=1\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, we can rewrite our expression using $(3),$ as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}tan^{2}⁡𝑥sec^{2}⁡𝑥−tan^{4}⁡𝑥+1 & =tan^{2}⁡𝑥(sec^{2}⁡𝑥−tan^{2}⁡𝑥)+1 \\ & =tan^{2}⁡𝑥⋅(1)+1 \\ & =tan^{2}⁡𝑥+1\end{aligned}



$$

Finally, we can use $(1),$ as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}tan^{2}⁡𝑥+1 & =sec^{2}⁡𝑥\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, we conclude that

$$



\tan^2 x\sec^2 x - \tan^4 x +1 = \sec^2 x.



$$
