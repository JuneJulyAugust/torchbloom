### Example: Factoring a Monomial out of a Binomial Containing One Variable

#### Question

Factor $4x^2+10x.$

#### Explanation

First, we find the $\textrm{GCF}$ of the two terms:

$$


\textrm{GCF}(4x^2,10x) = 2x


$$

Then, we rewrite each term in the original expression as a product with the $\textrm{GCF}\mathbin{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}4𝑥^{2}+10𝑥 & = \\ (2𝑥⋅2𝑥)+(2𝑥⋅5) & \end{aligned}


$$

Finally, we write the $\textrm{GCF}$ outside the parentheses and keep the remaining terms inside:

$$


\begin{aligned}(2𝑥⋅2𝑥)+(2𝑥⋅5) & = \\ 2𝑥(2𝑥+5) & \end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Factoring a Monomial out of a Binomial Containing Two Variables

#### Question

Factor $3t^2+12tw.$

#### Explanation

First, we find the $\textrm{GCF}$ of the two terms:

$$


\textrm{GCF}(3t^2,12tw) = 3t


$$

Then, we rewrite each term in the original expression as a product with the $\textrm{GCF}\mathbin{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}3𝑡^{2}+12𝑡𝑤 & = \\ (3𝑡⋅𝑡)+(3𝑡⋅4𝑤) & \end{aligned}


$$

Finally, we write the $\textrm{GCF}$ outside the parentheses and keep the remaining terms inside:

$$


\begin{aligned}(3𝑡⋅𝑡)+(3𝑡⋅4𝑤) & = \\ 3𝑡(𝑡+4𝑤) & \end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Factoring a Monomial From a Trinomial

#### Question

Factor $15x^2-5x-25xy.$

#### Explanation

First, we find the $\textrm{GCF}$ of the three terms:

$$


\textrm{GCF}(15x^2, 5x, 25xy) = 5x


$$

Then, we rewrite each term in the original expression as a product with the $\textrm{GCF}\mathbin{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}15𝑥^{2}−5𝑥−25𝑥𝑦 & = \\ (5𝑥⋅3𝑥)−(5𝑥⋅1)−(5𝑥⋅5𝑦) & \end{aligned}


$$

Finally, we write the $\textrm{GCF}$ outside the parentheses and keep the remaining terms inside:

$$


\begin{aligned}(5𝑥⋅3𝑥)−(5𝑥⋅1)−(5𝑥⋅5𝑦) & = \\ 5𝑥(3𝑥−1−5𝑦) & \end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Factoring a Binomial

#### Question

Factor the polynomial expression $8a(b^2-3)-c(b^2-3).$

#### Explanation

First, we find the $\textrm{GCF}$ of the two terms:

$$


\textrm{GCF}(8a(b^2-3),c(b^2-3)) = b^2-3


$$

Each term is already written as a product with the $\textrm{GCF}\mathbin{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}8𝑎⋅(𝑏^{2}−3)−𝑐⋅(𝑏^{2}−3)\end{aligned}


$$

Finally, we write the $\textrm{GCF}$ outside the parentheses and keep the remaining terms inside:

$$


\begin{aligned}8𝑎⋅(𝑏^{2}−3)−𝑐⋅(𝑏^{2}−3) & = \\ (8𝑎−𝑐)(𝑏^{2}−3) & \end{aligned}


$$
