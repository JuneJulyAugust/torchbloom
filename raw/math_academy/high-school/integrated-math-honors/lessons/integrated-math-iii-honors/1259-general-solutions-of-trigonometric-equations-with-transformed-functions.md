## Lesson

### Introduction

How can we find the general solution to the following trigonometric equation?

$$


\sin(2x-30^\circ) = \dfrac{1}{2}


$$

To find the general solution, we perform the following two steps:

- First, we find an implicit general solution by solving the equation, treating the expression inside the parentheses as a single variable.

- Then, we find an explicit general solution by solving the expressions obtained in the first step for the unknown variable.

So for this example, we let $X = 2x-30^\circ.$ Then, we have the equation

$$


\sin X = \dfrac{1}{2}.


$$

This equation has the general solution

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑋 & =30^{∘}+𝑛⋅360^{∘}, & \, & \, & 𝑋 & =150^{∘}+𝑛⋅360^{∘},\end{aligned}


$$

where $n$ is any integer.

Writing this general solution in terms of the original variable $x,$ we have

$$


\begin{aligned}2𝑥−30^{∘} & =30^{∘}+𝑛⋅360^{∘}, & \, & \, & 2𝑥−30^{∘} & =150^{∘}+𝑛⋅360^{∘}.\end{aligned}


$$

Solving the two equations above for $x,$ we get the following:

$$


\begin{aligned}2𝑥 & =60^{∘}+𝑛⋅360^{∘}, & \, & \, & 2𝑥 & =180^{∘}+𝑛⋅360^{∘} \\ 𝑥 & =30^{∘}+𝑛⋅180^{∘}, & \, & \, & 𝑥 & =90^{∘}+𝑛⋅180^{∘}\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, the general solution to our original equation is

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑥 & =30^{∘}+𝑛⋅180^{∘}, & \, & \, & 𝑥 & =90^{∘}+𝑛⋅180^{∘}.\end{aligned}


$$

Although we introduced an intermediate variable $X$ in this example, it's possible to skip this step. Let's see an example where we do this.

### Example: Finding the General Solution to an Equation Containing a Transformed Sine Function

#### Question

Find the general solution of the following trigonometric equation:

$$


\sin{\left(4x-90^\circ\right)} = -\dfrac{1}{2}


$$

#### Explanation

The general solution to our equation is

$$


\begin{aligned}4𝑥−90^{∘} & =−30^{∘}+360^{∘}𝑛, & \, & \, & 4𝑥−90^{∘} & =−150^{∘}+360^{∘}𝑛.\end{aligned}


$$

Solving the two equations above for $x,$ we get the following:

$$


\begin{aligned}4𝑥 & =90^{∘}−30^{∘}+360^{∘}𝑛 & \, & \, & 4𝑥 & =90^{∘}−150^{∘}+360^{∘}𝑛 \\ 4𝑥 & =60^{∘}+360^{∘}𝑛 & \, & \, & 4𝑥 & =−60^{∘}+360^{∘}𝑛 \\ 𝑥 & =15^{∘}+90^{∘}𝑛 & \, & \, & 𝑥 & =−15^{∘}+90^{∘}𝑛\end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Finding the General Solution to an Equation Containing a Transformed Cosine Function

#### Question

Find the general solution of the following trigonometric equation:

$$


\cos \left(6x + 75^\circ \right) = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}


$$

#### Explanation

The general solution to our equation is

$$


\begin{aligned}6𝑥+75^{∘} & =135^{∘}+𝑛⋅360^{∘}, & \, & \, & 6𝑥+75^{∘} & =225^{∘}+𝑛⋅360^{∘}.\end{aligned}


$$

Solving the two equations above for $x,$ we get the following:

$$


\begin{aligned}6𝑥 & =60^{∘}+𝑛⋅360^{∘} & \, & \, & 6𝑥 & =150^{∘}+𝑛⋅360^{∘} \\ 𝑥 & =10^{∘}+𝑛⋅60^{∘} & \, & \, & 𝑥 & =25^{∘}+𝑛⋅60^{∘}\end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Finding the General Solution to an Equation Containing a Transformed Tangent or Cotangent Function

#### Question

Find the general solution of the following trigonometric equation:

$$


\tan\left(2x - \dfrac{\pi}{4} \right) = -1


$$

#### Explanation

The general solution to our equation is

$$


\begin{aligned}2𝑥−\frac{𝜋}{4} & =−\frac{𝜋}{4}+𝑛𝜋.\end{aligned}


$$

Solving the above equation for $x,$ we get

$$


\begin{aligned}2𝑥 & =𝑛𝜋 \\ 𝑥 & =\frac{𝑛𝜋}{2}.\end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Finding the General Solution to an Equation Containing a Transformed Reciprocal Function

#### Question

Find the general solution of the following trigonometric equation:

$$


\sec\left(2x + \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right) = \sqrt{2}


$$

#### Explanation

First, note that the given equation is equivalent to

$$


\cos\left(2x + \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right) = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.


$$

Therefore, the general solution to our equation is

$$


2x + \dfrac{\pi}{2} = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2\pi n, \qquad\qquad 2x + \dfrac{\pi}{2} = \dfrac{7\pi}{4} + 2\pi n.


$$

Solving the two equations above for $x,$ we get the following:

$$


\begin{aligned}2𝑥=−\frac{𝜋}{2}+\frac{𝜋}{4}+2𝜋𝑛, & \, & \, & 2𝑥=−\frac{𝜋}{2}+\frac{7𝜋}{4}+2𝜋𝑛 \\ 2𝑥=−\frac{𝜋}{4}+2𝜋𝑛, & \, & \, & 2𝑥=\frac{5𝜋}{4}+2𝜋𝑛 \\ 𝑥=−\frac{𝜋}{8}+𝜋𝑛, & \, & \, & 𝑥=\frac{5𝜋}{8}+𝜋𝑛\end{aligned}


$$
