## Lesson

### Introduction

In this lesson, we will learn how to construct and solve two-step inequalities in context.

Suppose a cab driver charges her customers $10$ to drive them from the station to the hotel. Her total expenses amount to $20$ per day. What is the smallest number of rides she has to offer to earn at least $100$ per day?

The price per ride is $10.$ So, if the driver makes $n$ rides, she receives $10n$ dollars.

However, she spends $20$ every day on expenses. So her daily profit (in dollars) equals

$$



10n - 20.



$$

Since the driver wants to make *at least* $100$ per day, the following inequality must be satisfied:

$$



10n - 20 \geq 100



$$

Solving for $n,$ we obtain the following:

$$



\begin{aligned}10𝑛−20 & ≥100 \\ 10𝑛 & ≥100+20 \\ 10𝑛 & ≥120 \\ 𝑛 & ≥\frac{120}{10} \\ 𝑛 & ≥12\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, the driver must offer *at least* $12$ rides per day.

### Example: Modeling With Non-Strict Inequalities

#### Question

Anna wants to spend at most $16$ at the store. Each candy bar costs $2,$ and she also decides to buy one bottle of water for $6.$ Which of the following statements is correct?

1. Anna can buy at most $3$ candy bars.

2. Anna can buy at most $4$ candy bars.

3. Anna can buy at most $5$ candy bars.

#### Explanation

Each candy bar costs $2.$ So, if Anna buys $c$ candy bars, she will pay a total of $2c$ dollars.

However, she also decides to buy one bottle of water for $6.$ So, the total amount (in dollars) Anna must pay equals

$$



2c+6.



$$

Now, since Anna wants to spend ** $16,$ the following inequality must be satisfied:

$$



2c+6 \leq 16



$$

Solving for $c,$ we obtain the following:

$$



\begin{aligned}2𝑐+6 & ≤16 \\ 2𝑐 & ≤16−6 \\ 2𝑐 & ≤10 \\ 𝑐 & ≤\frac{10}{2} \\ 𝑐 & ≤5\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, Anna can buy ** $5$ candy bars.

So, the correct answer is "III only."

### Example: Modeling With Strict Inequalities

#### Question

Peter wants to spend less than $320$ to rent a van to go on a trip with his family. The rental company charges a flat rate of $70$ plus $50$ per day. Which of the following statements is correct?

1. Peter can rent the van for less than $6$ days.

2. Peter can rent the van for less than $5$ days.

3. Peter can rent the van for up to $6$ days.

#### Explanation

The price per day is $50.$ So, for $d$ days, Peter will pay $50d$ dollars.

However, there is also a flat fee of $70.$ So, the total (in dollars) Peter must pay equals

$$



50d+70.



$$

Since Peter wants to spend ** $320,$ the following inequality must be satisfied:

$$



50d+70 < 320



$$

Solving for $d,$ we obtain the following:

$$



\begin{aligned}50𝑑+70 & <320 \\ 50𝑑 & <320−70 \\ 50𝑑 & <250 \\ 𝑑 & <\frac{250}{50} \\ 𝑑 & <5\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, Peter can rent the van for ** $5$ days. So, the correct answer is "II only."

### Example: Interpreting Solutions With Fractions

#### Question

Vincent wants to watch some movies and spend at most $8.$ Each movie costs $1.40$, and Vincent plans to use a coupon that gives him $4$ off the final purchase. Which of the following statements is correct?

1. Vincent can watch at most $8$ movies.

2. Vincent can watch at most $9$ movies.

3. Vincent can watch up to $10$ movies.

#### Explanation

Each movie costs $1.40.$ So, if Vincent buys $n$ movies, he would normally pay $1.4n$ dollars.

However, Vincent will use a coupon that takes $4$ off the purchase price. So, Vincent will have to pay (in dollars) a total of

$$



1.4n-4.



$$

Since Vincent wants to spend ** $8.00,$ the following inequality must be satisfied:

$$



1.4n-4 \leq8



$$

Solving for $n,$ we obtain the following:

$$



\begin{aligned}1.4𝑛−4 & ≤8 \\ 1.4𝑛 & ≤8+4 \\ 1.4𝑛 & ≤12 \\ 𝑛 & ≤\frac{12}{1.4} \\ 𝑛 & ≤\frac{120}{14} \\ 𝑛 & ≤8\frac{4}{7}\end{aligned}



$$

Now, $n$ must be an integer. Therefore, we conclude that Vincent can watch ** $8$ movies.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only. "
