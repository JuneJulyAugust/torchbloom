## Lesson

### Introduction

To add complex numbers, we just add the real parts and add the imaginary parts separately. For example,

$$


\begin{aligned}(3+5i)+(2−3i) & =(3+5i)+(2−3i) \\ & =(3+2)+(5+(−\,3))i \\ & =(3+2)+(5−3)i \\ & =5+2i\end{aligned}


$$

In general, we write

$$


(a + b\textrm{i}) + (c + d\textrm{i}) = (a + c) + (b + d)\textrm{i}.


$$

Subtracting complex numbers works in the same way: we just subtract the real parts and subtract the imaginary parts separately. For example,

$$


\begin{aligned}(3+5i)−(2−3i) & =(3+5i)−(2−3i) \\ & =(3−2)+(5−(−\,3))i \\ & =(3−2)+(5+3)i \\ & =1+8i.\end{aligned}


$$

In general, we write

$$


(a + b\textrm{i}) - (c + d\textrm{i}) = (a - c) + (b - d)\textrm{i}.


$$

### Example: Adding Complex Numbers

#### Question

Evaluate $(4 + 3\,\textrm{i}) + (1 + 5\,\textrm{i}).$

#### Explanation

To add complex numbers, we just add the real parts and add the imaginary parts separately.

For the given problem, we have

$$


\begin{aligned}(4+3\,i)+(1+5\,i) & =(4+3\,i)+(1+5\,i) \\ & =(4+1)+(3+5)\,i \\ & =5+8\,i.\end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Subtracting Complex Numbers

#### Question

Find the value of $(5 + 4\,\textrm{i}) - (1 + 2\,\textrm{i}).$

#### Explanation

To subtract complex numbers, we just subtract the real parts and subtract the imaginary parts separately.

For the given problem, we have

$$


\begin{aligned}(5+4\,i)−(1+2\,i) & =(5+4\,i)−(1+2\,i) \\ & =(5−1)+(4−2)\,i \\ & =4+2\,i.\end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Adding and Subtracting Complex Numbers Written in Nonstandard Form

#### Question

Evaluate $\left(\dfrac{1}{2} + \sqrt{-9} \right) + (3 - \sqrt{-16}).$

#### Explanation

First, we simplify the two complex numbers:

$$


\begin{aligned}(\frac{1}{2}+\sqrt{√−9})+(3−\sqrt{√−16}) & =(\frac{1}{2}+3\,i)+(3−4\,i)\end{aligned}


$$

To add these two complex numbers, we just add the real parts and add the imaginary parts separately:

$$


\begin{aligned}(\frac{1}{2}+3\,i)+(3−4\,i) & =(\frac{1}{2}+3)+(3+(−4))\,i \\ & =(\frac{1}{2}+3)+(3−4)\,i \\ & =\frac{7}{2}−1\,i \\ & =\frac{7}{2}−\,i\end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Adding Purely Imaginary Numbers

#### Question

Find the value of $5\,\textrm{i} - 8\,\textrm{i}.$

#### Explanation

To subtract two complex numbers, we just subtract the real parts and subtract the imaginary parts separately.

In this case, $5\,\textrm{i}$ and $8\,\textrm{i}$ are purely imaginary numbers. Therefore, we only need to subtract their imaginary parts, as follows:

$$


\begin{aligned}5\,i−8\,i=(5−8)\,i & =−3\,i\end{aligned}


$$
