## Lesson

### Introduction

To divide one rational expression by another, we multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor. Just like with fractions!

For example, let's divide $x$ by $\dfrac{3}{y}\mathbin{:}$

$$


x \div \dfrac{3}{y}


$$

To do this, we just need to multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor. The divisor is $\dfrac{3}{y},$ and to get the reciprocal, we just flip the fraction upside down: $\dfrac{y}{3}.$ Multiplying by the reciprocal of the divisor, we get

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑥÷\frac{3}{𝑦} & = \\ 𝑥⋅\frac{𝑦}{3} & = \\ \frac{𝑥𝑦}{3} & .\end{aligned}


$$

Let's see some more examples!

### Example: Dividing Rational Expressions with No Common Factors

#### Question

What is the result of dividing $\dfrac 3 x$ by $\dfrac {5}{y}?$

#### Explanation

To compute

$$


\dfrac{3}{x} \div \dfrac{5}{y},


$$

we just need to multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor:

$$


\begin{aligned} \dfrac 3 x \div \dfrac 5 y&=\\\dfrac 3 x \cdot \dfrac y 5&=\\\dfrac {3y} {5x} \end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Dividing Rational Expressions with a Single Common Factor

#### Question

What is the result of dividing $\dfrac 3 x$ by $\dfrac y {6x^2}?$

#### Explanation

To compute

$$


\dfrac{3}{x} \div \dfrac{y}{6x^2},


$$

we just need to multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor:

$$


 \begin{aligned} \dfrac 3 x \div \dfrac y {6x^2} &=\\\dfrac 3 x \cdot \dfrac {6x^2} y &=\\\dfrac {18x^2} {xy}&=\\\dfrac {18x^2} {xy}&=\\\dfrac {18x} {y}& \end{aligned}


$$

Note that in the final step, we canceled out a common factor of $x$ in the numerator and denominator.

### Example: Dividing Rational Expressions with Multiple Common Factors

#### Question

Given that $\dfrac {2a^2(1+b)} {3b} =x$ and $\dfrac {1+b}{5b^2} =y,$ then what is $x \div y?$

#### Explanation

To compute $x \div y,$ i.e.,

$$


\begin{aligned}\frac{2𝑎^{2}(1+𝑏)}{3𝑏} & ÷\frac{1+𝑏}{5𝑏^{2}},\end{aligned}


$$

we just need to multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor:

$$


 \begin{aligned} \dfrac {2a^2(1+b)} {3b} \div \dfrac{1+b}{5b^2}&=\\\[5pt] \dfrac {2a^2(1+b)} {3b} \cdot \dfrac {5b^2} {1+b}&=\\\[5pt] \dfrac {2a^2(1+b)} {3b} \cdot \dfrac {5b^{2}} {1+b}&=\\\[5pt] \dfrac {10a^2b} {3} \end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Dividing Rational Expressions and Simplifying the Result by Factoring

#### Question

Given that $\dfrac {2a^3 - 10a} {7b} =x$ and $\dfrac {2a} {14b^2}=y,$ then what is $x \div y?$

#### Explanation

To compute

$$


 \begin{aligned} \dfrac {2a^3 - 10a} {7b} &\div \dfrac {2a} {14b^2}, \end{aligned}


$$

we just need to multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor:

$$


 \begin{aligned} \dfrac {2a^3 - 10a} {7b} \div \dfrac {2a} {14b^2}&=\\\dfrac {2a^3 - 10a} {7b} \cdot \dfrac {14b^2} {2a}&=\\\dfrac {2a(a^2 - 5)} {7b} \cdot \dfrac {2b \cdot 7b} {2a}&=\\\dfrac {2a(a^2 - 5)} {7b} \cdot \dfrac {2b \cdot 7b} {2a}&=\\\dfrac {2b(a^2 - 5)} 1 &=\\2a^2b - 10b & \end{aligned}


$$
