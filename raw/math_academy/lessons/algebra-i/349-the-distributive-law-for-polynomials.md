### Example: Multiplying Polynomials

#### Question

Simplify $-3t^3\left(3t^2+2t\right).$

#### Explanation

We multiply each term inside the parentheses by the term outside the parentheses, simplify using the rules of exponents, and then we add the results together:

$$


\begin{aligned}−3𝑡^{3}(3𝑡^{2}+2𝑡) & = \\ (−3𝑡^{3})⋅(3𝑡^{2})+(−3𝑡^{3})⋅(2𝑡) & = \\ ((−3)⋅3)(𝑡^{3}⋅𝑡^{2})+((−3)⋅2)(𝑡^{3}⋅𝑡) & = \\ (−9)(𝑡^{3+2})+(−6)(𝑡^{3+1}) & = \\ (−9)(𝑡^{5})+(−6)(𝑡^{4}) & = \\ −9𝑡^{5}−6𝑡^{4} & \end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Cases Containing Two Negative Signs

#### Question

Simplify $-5x\left(x^3-4\right).$

#### Explanation

We multiply each term inside the parentheses by the term outside the parentheses, simplify using the rules of exponents, and then we add the results together:

$$


\begin{aligned}−5𝑥(𝑥^{3}−4) & = \\ (−5𝑥)(𝑥^{3})+(−5𝑥)(−4) & = \\ (−5)(𝑥⋅𝑥^{3})+((−5)⋅(−4))(𝑥) & = \\ (−5)𝑥^{1+3}+(20)𝑥 & = \\ −5𝑥^{4}+20𝑥 & \end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Multiplying Polynomials Containing Fractions

#### Question

Simplify $-6x^2\left(\dfrac{x^3}{2}+3x^2\right).$

#### Explanation

We multiply each term inside the parentheses by the term outside the parentheses, simplify using the rules of exponents, and then we add the results together:

$$


\begin{aligned}−6𝑥^{2}(\frac{𝑥^{3}}{2}+3𝑥^{2}) & = \\ (−6𝑥^{2})(\frac{𝑥^{3}}{2})+(−6𝑥^{2})(3𝑥^{2}) & = \\ (−6𝑥^{2})(\frac{1}{2}𝑥^{3})+(−6𝑥^{2})(3𝑥^{2}) & = \\ ((−6)⋅\frac{1}{2})(𝑥^{3}⋅𝑥^{2})+((−6)⋅3)(𝑥^{2}⋅𝑥^{2}) & = \\ (−3)(𝑥^{3+2})+(−18)(𝑥^{2+2}) & = \\ −3𝑥^{5}−18𝑥^{4} & \end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Multiplying Multivariable Polynomials

#### Question

Simplify $xy\left(3x^2+xy+4\right).$

#### Explanation

We multiply each term inside the parentheses by the term outside the parentheses, simplify using the rules of exponents, and then we add the results together:

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑥𝑦(3𝑥^{2}+𝑥𝑦+4) & = \\ (𝑥𝑦)⋅(3𝑥^{2})+(𝑥𝑦)⋅(𝑥𝑦)+(𝑥𝑦)⋅4 & = \\ 3(𝑥⋅𝑥^{2})(𝑦)+(𝑥⋅𝑥)(𝑦⋅𝑦)+4(𝑥𝑦) & = \\ 3(𝑥^{3})(𝑦)+(𝑥^{2})(𝑦^{2})+4𝑥𝑦 & = \\ 3𝑥^{3}𝑦+𝑥^{2}𝑦^{2}+4𝑥𝑦 & \end{aligned}


$$
