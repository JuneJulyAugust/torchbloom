## Lesson

### Introduction

To factor a polynomial, the first step is to find the common factor of all the terms and factor it out. Once we've done that, we can try to apply other factoring tricks that we've learned for quadratic expressions.

For example, consider the polynomial $2x^3-2x.$

First, notice that all of the terms have a common factor of $2x.$ To simplify the computation, we can factor out the $2x,$ as follows:

$$


2x^3-2x = 2x(x^2-1)


$$

Now, we factor the expression $x^2-1$ using the difference of squares formula:

$$


2x(x^2-1) = 2x(x+1)(x-1)


$$

So, the expression $2x^3-2x$ factors into $2x(x+1)(x-1).$

### Example: Factoring Using Common Factors and Differences of Squares

#### Question

Factor the expression $x^3 - 4x.$

#### Explanation

First, notice that all of the terms have a common factor of $x.$ To simplify the computation, we can factor out the $x,$ as follows:

$$


x^3 - 4x = x(x^2 - 4)


$$

Now, we factor the expression $x^2 - 4$ using the difference of squares formula:

$$


x(x^2 - 4) = x(x+2)(x-2)


$$

### Example: Factoring Using Common Factors and Perfect Square Trinomials

#### Question

Factor the expression $x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x.$

#### Explanation

First, notice that all of the terms have a common factor of $x.$ To simplify the computation, we can factor out the $x,$ as follows:

$$


x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x = x(x^2 - 6x + 9)


$$

The expression $x^2 - 6x + 9$ is a perfect square trinomial that can be factored as follows:

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑥(𝑥^{2}−6𝑥+9) & = \\ 𝑥(𝑥^{2}−6𝑥+3^{2}) & = \\ 𝑥(𝑥^{2}−2⋅3𝑥+3^{2}) & = \\ 𝑥(𝑥−3)^{2} & \end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Factoring Using Common Factors and Quadratic Factoring

#### Question

Factor the expression $x^3 + x^2 - 2x.$

#### Explanation

Note that all of the terms have a common factor of $x.$ To simplify the computation, we can factor out the $x,$ as follows:

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑥^{3}+𝑥^{2}−2𝑥 & =𝑥(𝑥^{2}+𝑥−2)\end{aligned}


$$

Now, we factor the polynomial $x^2 + x - 2$ as follows:

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑥(𝑥^{2}+𝑥−2) & = \\ 𝑥((𝑥^{2}−𝑥)+(2𝑥−2)) & = \\ 𝑥(𝑥(𝑥−1)+2(𝑥−1)) & = \\ 𝑥(𝑥+2)(𝑥−1) & \end{aligned}


$$
