## Lesson

### Introduction

Suppose we want to find all of the solutions to the equation

$$


x \cdot 3^x - 2 \cdot 3^x = 0.


$$

First, we factor the equation:

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑥⋅3^{𝑥}−2⋅3^{𝑥} & =0 \\ (𝑥−2)⋅3^{𝑥} & =0\end{aligned}


$$

We can now apply the zero product property, which states that $ab=0$ if and only if $a=0$ or $b=0.$ This gives us two equations,

$$


x-2 = 0 \quad \textrm{and} \quad 3^x=0.


$$

We now solve each of these equations separately.

- The equation $x-2=0$ has the solution $x=2.$

- The equation $3^x=0$ has no real solution because $3^x$ is positive for every real number $x.$

Therefore, the original equation has a single solution, $x=2.$

### Example: Solving an Exponential Equation by Factoring

#### Question

What is the product of solutions of the equation $x^2e^{x-1} - 2x e^{x-1} -15 e^{x-1} = 0?$

#### Explanation

First, we factor the equation:

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑥^{2}𝑒^{𝑥−1}−2𝑥𝑒^{𝑥−1}−15𝑒^{𝑥−1} & =0 \\ (𝑥^{2}−2𝑥−15)𝑒^{𝑥−1} & =0\end{aligned}


$$

We can now apply the zero product property, which states that $ab=0$ if and only if $a=0$ or $b=0.$ This gives us two equations,

$$


x^2 - 2x - 15 = 0 \quad \textrm{and} \quad e^{x-1}=0.


$$

We now solve each of these equations separately.

- The equation $x^2 - 2x - 15 = 0$ can be factored as which leads to the solutions $x=-3,5.$

- The equation $e^{x-1}=0$ has no solutions because $e^{x-1}$ is positive for every real number $x.$

Therefore, the original equation has the solutions $x=-3,5,$ and their product is $(-3) \cdot 5 = -15.$

### Example: Solving an Exponential Equation by Rearranging the Equation and Then Factoring

#### Question

What is the product of solutions of the equation $9x^2\cdot 3^{x+1} = 16\cdot 3^{x+1}?$

#### Explanation

First, we factor the equation:

$$


\begin{aligned}9𝑥^{2}⋅3^{𝑥+1} & =16⋅3^{𝑥+1} \\ 9𝑥^{2}⋅3^{𝑥+1}−16⋅3^{𝑥+1} & =0 \\ (9𝑥^{2}−16)3^{𝑥+1} & =0\end{aligned}


$$

We can now apply the zero product property, which states that $ab=0$ if and only if $a=0$ or $b=0.$ This gives us two equations,

$$


9x^2 - 16 = 0 \quad \textrm{and} \quad 3^{x+1}=0.


$$

We now solve each of these equations separately.

- The equation $9x^2 - 16=0$ can be factored as which leads to the solutions $x=-\dfrac{4}{3},\dfrac{4}{3}.$

- The equation $3^{x+1}=0$ has no solutions because $3^{x+1}$ is positive for every real number $x.$

Therefore, the original equation has the solutions $x=-\dfrac{4}{3},\dfrac{4}{3},$ and their product is $\left(-\dfrac{4}{3} \right) \cdot \dfrac{4}{3} = -\dfrac{16}{9}.$
