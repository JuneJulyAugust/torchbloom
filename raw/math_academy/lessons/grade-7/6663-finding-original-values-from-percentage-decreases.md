## Lesson

### Introduction

In a previous lesson, we saw how to apply a percentage decrease to a number to get a new, smaller number. In this lesson, we’ll work backwards, finding the *original value* when we know the percentage decrease and the new value.

For example, suppose that after a $5\%$ decrease, a conference registration fee is $228.$ What was the original fee?

Decreasing a number by $5\%$ means that we must apply a new percentage of

$$



100\% - 5\% = 95\%.



$$

Converting this percentage to a decimal, we have

$$



95\%=\frac{95}{100}=0.95.



$$

Now, let the original fee be $x.$ Then, after a $5\%$ decrease, the new value is

$$



0.95x.



$$

We are told that the decreased value is $228.$ So, we set them equal and solve for $x{:}$

$$



\begin{aligned}0.95𝑥 & =228 \\ 𝑥 & =\frac{228}{0.95} \\ 𝑥 & =240\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, the original fee was $240.$

We can check that this answer is correct by applying a $5\%$ decrease to $240{:}$

$$



\begin{aligned}240×(100\%−5\%) & =240×95\% \\ & =240×0.95 \\ & =228\,✓\end{aligned}



$$

### Example: Finding an Original Value Given a Percentage Decrease

#### Question

After decreasing a quantity $x$ by $16\%,$ the result is $84.$ What is the value of $x?$

#### Explanation

Decreasing a number by $16\%$ means that we must apply a percentage of

$$



100\% - 16\% = 84\%.



$$

Converting this percentage to a decimal, we have

$$



84\% = \dfrac{84}{100} = 0.84.



$$

We're told that by decreasing a number $x$ by $16\%,$ we get the number $84.$ So, we have the equation

$$



0.84x = 84.



$$

Now, we solve for $x{:}$

$$



\begin{aligned}𝑥 & =\frac{84}{0.84} \\ & =100\end{aligned}



$$

So, we have $x = 100.$

### Example: Finding an Original Value Given a Percentage Decrease: Word Problems

#### Question

After a $15\%$ discount, the price of a smartphone is $425.$ What was the original price?

#### Explanation

Decreasing a number by $15\%$ means that we must apply a new percentage of

$$



100\% - 15\% = 85\%.



$$

Converting this percentage to a decimal, we have

$$



85\% = \dfrac{85}{100} = 0.85.



$$

Let the smartphone's original price be $x.$ So, after a $15\%$ decrease, the new value is

$$



0.85x.



$$

We are told the discounted price is $425.$ So, we have the equation

$$



0.85x = 425.



$$

Now, we solve for $x,$ as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}0.85𝑥 & =425 \\ 𝑥 & =\frac{425}{0.85} \\ 𝑥 & =500\end{aligned}



$$

So, the original price was $500.$

### Example: Finding a Total Decrease Given a Percentage Decrease

#### Question

A collector sold part of a marble collection. After $45\%$ of the original marbles were sold, $484$ marbles remained. How many marbles were sold?

#### Explanation

Decreasing a number by $45\%$ means that we must apply a new percentage of

$$



100\% - 45\% = 55\%.



$$

Converting this percentage to a decimal, we have

$$



55\% = \dfrac{55}{100} = 0.55.



$$

Let the original number of marbles be $x.$ So, after a $45\%$ decrease, the new value is

$$



0.55x.



$$

We are told that $484$ marbles remained. So, we have the equation

$$



0.55x = 484.



$$

Now, we solve for $x{:}$

$$



\begin{aligned}0.55𝑥 & =484 \\ 𝑥 & =\frac{484}{0.55} \\ 𝑥 & =880\end{aligned}



$$

Now that we know the original number of marbles was $880,$ we find the number removed by taking $45\%$ of $880{:}$

$$



\dfrac{45}{100} \cdot 880 = 396.



$$

So, $396$ marbles were sold.
