## Lesson

### Introduction

To multiply a binomial and a trinomial, we take each term of the binomial and multiply it by every term in the trinomial. Then, we simplify by collecting like terms.

For example, to multiply $({\color{blue}{x}} + {\color{red}{3}})$ and $(x^2 + 2x+4),$ we multiply ${\color{blue}{x}}$ and $(x^2 + 2x+4),$ and then we add this to the product of ${\color{red}{3}}$ and $(x^2 + 2x+4).$

$$



\begin{aligned}(𝑥+3)(𝑥^{2}+2𝑥+4) & = \\ 𝑥(𝑥^{2}+2𝑥+4)+3(𝑥^{2}+2𝑥+4) & = \\ 𝑥^{3}+2𝑥^{2}+4𝑥+3𝑥^{2}+6𝑥+12 & \end{aligned}



$$

Then, we group the like terms and simplify:

$$



\begin{aligned}𝑥^{3}+2𝑥^{2}+4𝑥+3𝑥^{2}+6𝑥+12 & = \\ 𝑥^{3}+2𝑥^{2}+3𝑥^{2}+4𝑥+6𝑥+12 & = \\ 𝑥^{3}+5𝑥^{2}+10𝑥+12 & \end{aligned}



$$

### Example: Multiplying a Binomial and a Quadratic Trinomial

#### Question

Simplify the product $(x - 3)(x^2 - 2x + 6).$

#### Explanation

We distribute both terms of the binomial to the trinomial in the second parenthesis. Then, we simplify.

$$



\begin{aligned} (x - 3)(x^2 - 2x + 6) &= \\x(x^2 - 2x + 6) - 3(x^2 - 2x + 6) &= \\x^3 - 2x^2 + 6x - 3x^2 + 6x - 18 &= \\x^3 - 5x^2 + 12x - 18 \end{aligned}



$$

### Example: Multiplying Trinomials

#### Question

Given that $(3s^2 + 2s - 1)(s^2 + s + 2) = 3s^4 + \boxed{a}s^3 + \boxed{b}s^2 + \boxed{c}s - 2,$ calculate $a+b+c.$

#### Explanation

We distribute all three terms of the trinomial in the first set of parentheses to the trinomial in the second set of parentheses. Then, we simplify.

$$



\begin{aligned}(3𝑠^{2}+2𝑠−1)(𝑠^{2}+𝑠+2) & = \\ 3𝑠^{2}(𝑠^{2}+𝑠+2)+2𝑠(𝑠^{2}+𝑠+2)+(−1)(𝑠^{2}+𝑠+2) & = \\ 3𝑠^{4}+3𝑠^{3}+6𝑠^{2}+2𝑠^{3}+2𝑠^{2}+4𝑠−𝑠^{2}−𝑠−2 & = \\ 3𝑠^{4}+5𝑠^{3}+7𝑠^{2}+3𝑠−2 & \end{aligned}



$$

Matching up the coefficients, we find that $a=5,$ $b=7,$ and $c=3.$

So, we have

$$



a + b + c = 5 + 7 + 3 = 15.



$$

### Example: Multiplying Binomials and Trinomials Containing More Than One Variable

#### Question

What is the coefficient of $a^2b$ in the product $(a+b)(a^2 + ab + b^2)?$

#### Explanation

We distribute both terms of the binomial in the first set of parentheses to the trinomial in the second set of parentheses. Then, we simplify.

$$



\begin{aligned}(𝑎+𝑏)(𝑎^{2}+𝑎𝑏+𝑏^{2}) & = \\ 𝑎(𝑎^{2}+𝑎𝑏+𝑏^{2})+𝑏(𝑎^{2}+𝑎𝑏+𝑏^{2}) & = \\ 𝑎^{3}+𝑎^{2}𝑏+𝑎𝑏^{2}+𝑎^{2}𝑏+𝑎𝑏^{2}+𝑏^{3} & = \\ 𝑎^{3}+2𝑎^{2}𝑏+2𝑎𝑏^{2}+𝑏^{3} & \end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, the coefficient of $a^2 b$ is $2.$
