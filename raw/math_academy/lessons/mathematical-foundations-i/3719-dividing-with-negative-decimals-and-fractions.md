## Lesson

### Introduction

Let's remind ourselves of the three rules for dividing with negative numbers:

- A negative number divided by a positive number gives a negative number.

- A positive number divided by a negative number is also a negative number.

- A negative number divided by a negative number is a positive number.

### A Worked Example

As an example, let's find the value of

$$


2.4 \div (-4).


$$

Dividing a positive number by a negative number gives a negative number. Hence, the result will be negative.

Therefore, since

$$


2.4\div 4=0.6,


$$

we obtain

$$


2.4 \div (-4) = -0.6.


$$

### Example: Dividing With Negative Decimals

#### Question

Calculate $2.4 \div (-1.2).$

#### Explanation

Dividing a positive number by a negative number gives a negative number. Hence, the result will be negative.

Therefore, since

$$


2.4\div 1.2 = 2,


$$

we obtain

$$


2.4 \div (-1.2) = -2.


$$

### Example: Dividing With Negative Whole Numbers and Fractions

#### Question

Find the value of $\dfrac{4}{7} \div (-2).$

#### Explanation

Dividing a positive number by a negative number gives a negative number. Hence, the result will be negative.

Therefore, since

$$


\dfrac{4}{7} \div 2 = \dfrac{4}{7 \times 2} = \dfrac{4}{14} = \dfrac{2}{7},


$$

we obtain

$$


\dfrac{4}{7} \div (-2) = -\dfrac {2}{7}.


$$

### Example: Dividing With Negative Fractions

#### Question

Find the value of $\left(-\dfrac{2}{5}\right) \div \dfrac{1}{10}.$

#### Explanation

Dividing a negative number by a positive number gives a negative number. Hence, the result will be negative.

Therefore, since

$$


\dfrac{2}{5} \div \dfrac{1}{10} = \dfrac {2 \times 10}{5 \times 1} = \dfrac{20}{5} =4,


$$

we obtain

$$


\left(-\dfrac{2}{5}\right) \div \dfrac{1}{10} = -4.


$$
