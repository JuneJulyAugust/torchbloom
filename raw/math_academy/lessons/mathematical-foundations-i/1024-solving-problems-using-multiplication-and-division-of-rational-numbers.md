## Lesson

### Introduction

Some real-world situations involve a quantity changing by the same amount repeatedly.

When the same change occurs repeatedly, we can represent the total change using multiplication.

For example, suppose a submarine begins at sea level and descends $2\,\text{m}$ each minute for $4$ minutes.

Since descending represents a decrease in elevation, the change each minute is $-2\,\text{m}.$

The total change can be written as repeated subtraction:

$$


4


$$

This repeated subtraction can also be written using multiplication:

$$


{\color{blue}4} \times (-2)


$$

Evaluating the product, we get

$$


4 \times (-2) = -8.


$$

Therefore, after $4$ minutes, the submarine's elevation is $-8\,\text{m}.$

### Example: Solving Problems Involving Multiplying Negative Numbers

#### Question

A bank account allows overdrafts. A customer opens an account and immediately withdraws $3.40$ each day for $5$ days. What is the account balance after $5$ days?

#### Explanation

The bank balance decreases by $3.40$ each day.

Since the change is a decrease and occurs for $5$ days, the total change can be written as repeated subtraction of $3.40{:}$

$$


\underbrace{-\;3.40 - 3.40 - 3.40 - 3.40 - 3.40}_{\text{\color{blue}5 times}}


$$

Repeated subtraction can also be written using multiplication:

$$


{\color{blue}5} \times (-3.40)


$$

Evaluating the product, we get

$$


5 \times (-3.40) = -17.


$$

Therefore, the account balance after $5$ days is $-17.00.$

### Example: Solving Problems Involving Multiplying Negative Fractions

#### Question

The temperature of a liquid decreases by $\dfrac{4}{5}\,^\circ\text{C}$ each minute for $\dfrac{3}{2}$ minutes. What is the total change in temperature?

#### Explanation

The temperature decreases by $\dfrac{4}{5}\,^\circ\text{C}$ each minute.

Since the temperature decreases, the change each minute is $-\dfrac{4}{5}.$

The change occurs for $\dfrac{3}{2}$ minutes, so the total change is

$$


\dfrac{3}{2} \times \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}\right).


$$

Evaluating the product, we get

$$


\dfrac{3}{2} \times \left(-\dfrac{4}{5}\right) = -\dfrac{3 \times 4}{2 \times 5} = -\dfrac{12}{10} = -\dfrac{6}{5}.


$$

Therefore, the total change in temperature is $-\dfrac{6}{5}\,^\circ\text{C}.$

### Example: Solving Problems Involving Multiplying Negative Fractions and Mixed Numbers

#### Question

A submarine descends $1\,\dfrac{3}{8}\,\text{m}$ each minute from sea level. What is the submarine's elevation after $\dfrac{8}{3}$ minutes?

#### Explanation

The submarine descends $1\,\dfrac{3}{8}\,\text{m}$ each minute.

Since the elevation decreases, the change each minute is $-1\,\dfrac{3}{8}.$

The descent lasts $\dfrac{8}{3}$ minutes, so the total change in elevation is

$$


\dfrac{8}{3} \times \left(-1\,\dfrac{3}{8}\right).


$$

First, we write the mixed number as an improper fraction:

$$


-1\,\dfrac{3}{8} = -\dfrac{1\times8 + 3}{8} = -\dfrac{11}{8}


$$

So the expression becomes

$$


\dfrac{8}{3} \times \left(-\dfrac{11}{8}\right).


$$

Now, we evaluate the product:

$$


\dfrac{8}{3} \times \left(-\dfrac{11}{8}\right) = -\dfrac{8 \times 11}{3 \times 8} = -\dfrac{11}{3}


$$

Therefore, the submarine's elevation after $\dfrac{8}{3}$ minutes is $-\dfrac{11}{3}\,\text{m}.$

### Solving Problems Using Division of Rational Numbers

Sometimes we know the total change in a quantity and want to find how much the quantity changes during each equal time period.

In these situations, we use division.

For example, suppose a total of $24$ is withdrawn from a bank account over $6$ days in equal amounts.

Since money is withdrawn, the total change in the balance is $-24$.

To find the change each day, we divide the total change by the number of days:

$$


-24 \div 6


$$

Evaluating the quotient, we get

$$


-24 \div 6 = -4.


$$

Therefore, the balance changes by $-4$ each day.

### Example: Solving Problems Involving Dividing Negative Numbers

#### Question

Over the course of $6$ hours, the temperature of a liquid decreases by $9.6^\circ\text{C}$ in total. Assuming the temperature decreases at a constant rate, what is the change in temperature each hour?

#### Explanation

Since the temperature decreases by $9.6^\circ\text{C}$ over $6$ hours, the total change is $-9.6^\circ\text{C}.$

This change occurs evenly over $6$ hours, so we divide the total change by $6{:}$

$$


-9.6 \div 6


$$

Evaluating the quotient, we get

$$


-9.6 \div 6 = -1.6.


$$

Therefore, the temperature changes by $-1.6^\circ\text{C}$ each hour.
