### Introduction

Let's calculate the following sum:

$$
410 + 352
$$

We proceed using the standard algorithm.

First, we line up our numbers (ones over ones, and tens over tens):

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Adding the ones, we get ${\color{blue}0} + {\color{blue}2} = {\color{blue}2}{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2.** Adding the tens, we get ${\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}5} = {\color{blue}6}{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 3.** Adding the hundreds, we get ${\color{blue}4} + {\color{blue}3} = {\color{blue}7}{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $410 + 352 = 762.$

### Example: Adding Three-Digit Numbers Without Carrying

#### Question

There are $373$ boys and $415$ girls in a particular elementary school. What is the total number of students in the school?

#### Explanation

To find the total number of students, we need to calculate the value of

$$
373+415.
$$

First, we line up our numbers (** over ** and ** over **):

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**** Adding the ones, ${\color{blue}3} + {\color{blue}5} = {\color{blue}8}{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

**** Adding the tens, ${\color{blue}7} + {\color{blue}1} = {\color{blue}8}{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

**** Adding the hundreds, ${\color{blue}3} + {\color{blue}4} = {\color{blue}7}{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

So, $373 + 415 = 788.$

Therefore, there are a total of $788$ students in the school.

### Addition With Carrying

Let's calculate the following sum:

$$
128 + 249
$$

First, we line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones* and *tens* over *tens*):

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Adding the ones, we get ${\color{blue}8} + {\color{blue}9} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}7}.$ We write ${\color{blue}7}$ below the *ones* and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the *tens*:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2.** Adding the tens, we get ${\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}2} + {\color{blue}4} = {\color{blue}7}{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 3.** Adding the hundreds, we get ${\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}2} = {\color{blue}3}{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $128 + 249 = 377.$

### Example: Adding Three-Digit Numbers With Carrying

#### Question

What is $850 + 645?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers (** over ** and ** over **):

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**** Adding the ones, ${\color{blue}0} + {\color{blue}5} = {\color{blue}5}{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

**** Adding the tens, ${\color{blue}5} + {\color{blue}4} = {\color{blue}9}{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

**** Adding the hundreds, ${\color{blue}8} + {\color{blue}6} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}4}.$ We write ${\color{blue}4}$ below the ** and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the **:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & & & \\ & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

**** Adding the thousands, ${\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}0} + {\color{blue}0} = {\color{blue}1}{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & & & \\ & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $850 + 645 = 1,495.$

### Example: Adding Three-Digit Numbers: Carrying Multiple Times

#### Question

Calculate $457 + 789.$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers (** over ** and ** over **):

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**** Adding the ones, ${\color{blue}7} + {\color{blue}9} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}6}.$ We write ${\color{blue}6}$ below the ** and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the **:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

**** Adding the tens, ${\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}5} + {\color{blue}8} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}4}.$ We write ${\color{blue}4}$ below the ** and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the **:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

**** Adding the hundreds, ${\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}4} + {\color{blue}7} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}2}.$ We write ${\color{blue}2}$ below the ** and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the **:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

**** Adding the thousands, ${\color{blue}1}+{\color{blue}0}+{\color{blue}0}={\color{blue}1}{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $457 + 789 = 1,246.$
