## Lesson

### Introduction

Let's compute the following sum:

$$
4,745,136 + 25,985
$$

We proceed, as usual, using the standard algorithm.

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we add the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$
\begin{aligned} & & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $4,745,136 + 25,985 = 4,771,121.$

### Example: Adding Numbers Up to Seven Digits Without Carrying

#### Question

Calculate the sum $5,326,143 + 271,612.$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $5,326,143 + 271,612 = 5,597,755.$

### Example: Adding Numbers Up to Seven Digits With Carrying

#### Question

What is $3,246,341+ 71,769?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$
\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $3,246,341+ 71,769 = 3,318,110.$

### Example: Adding Numbers Up to Seven Digits: Word Problems

#### Question

In $2020$, a certain country had an elderly population of $2,079,439$ and a non-elderly population of $8,019,826.$ What was the total population of the country?

#### Explanation

To find the total population of the country, we need to calculate the value of

$$
2,079,439 + 8,019,826.
$$

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$
\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

So, $2,079,439 + 8,019,826 = 10,099,265.$

Therefore, the total population of the country was $10,099,265.$
