## Lesson

### Introduction

Let's compute the following difference:

$$
1,456,546 - 1,224,292
$$

As always, the first step is to line up our numbers according to their place value (ones over ones, tens over tens, hundreds over hundreds, and so on):

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left, borrowing where needed.

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\, & \,\,\, & \,\,\, & \,\,\, & \,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 4\end{aligned} & \,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 14\end{aligned} & \,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $1,456,546 - 1,224,292 = 232,254.$

### Example: Subtracting Numbers Up to Seven Digits Without Borrowing

#### Question

Compute the value of $1,487,645 - 45,214.$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,8\,\,\, & \,\,\,7\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,8\,\,\, & \,\,\,7\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,1\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
1,487,645 - 45,214 = 1,442,431.
$$

### Example: Subtracting Numbers Up to Seven Digits With Borrowing

#### Question

What is $2,354,702 - 1,446,254?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,7\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left, borrowing where needed.

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 1\end{aligned} & \,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 13\end{aligned} & \,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 4\end{aligned} & \,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 14\end{aligned} & \,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 6\end{aligned} & \,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 9\end{aligned} & \,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 12\end{aligned} \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,9\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,8\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,8\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
2,354,702 - 1,446,254 = 908,448.
$$

### Example: Subtracting Numbers Up to Seven Digits: Word Problems

#### Question

Mike, a baseball player, earns $\[math]1, 245,800$ per year, while Randy, a football player, makes $\[/math]1,227,900$ per year. How much more does Mike earn per year compared to Randy?

#### Explanation

To find out how much more Mike earns compared to Randy, we need to calculate the value of

$$
1, 245,800 - 1,227,900.
$$

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,8\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,7\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left, borrowing where needed.

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\, & \,\,\, & \,\begin{aligned} \\ 3\end{aligned} & \,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 14\end{aligned} & \,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 18\end{aligned} & \,\,\, & \,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,7\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, \\ & & & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,7\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, Mike earns $\[math]17,900$ more per year.
