### Introduction

Using the standard algorithm, we can calculate the difference between two numbers containing any number of digits.

Let's use the standard algorithm to compute the following difference:

$$
42,345 - 3,124
$$

First, we line up our numbers (ones over ones, tens over tens, hundreds over hundreds, and so on):

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left, borrowing where needed.

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 3\end{aligned} & \,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 12\end{aligned} & \,\,\, & \,\,\, & \,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,1\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
42,345 - 3,124 = 39,221.
$$

### Example: Subtracting Numbers Up to Five Digits Without Borrowing

#### Question

Compute the value of $57,458 - 25,242.$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,7\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,8\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,7\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,8\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
57,458 - 25,242 = 32,216.
$$

### Example: Subtracting Numbers Up to Five Digits With Borrowing

#### Question

What is $83,005 - 52,009?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,8\,\,\, & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left, borrowing where needed.

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\, & \,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 2\end{aligned} & \,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 9\end{aligned} & \,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 9\end{aligned} & \,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 15\end{aligned} \\ & & \,\,\,8\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,5\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
83,005 - 52,009 = 30,996.
$$

### Example: Subtracting Numbers Up to Five Digits: Word Problems

#### Question

There are $32,355$ bees in a hive. On a particular day, $12, 247$ of the bees leave to find pollen. How many bees are left in the hive?

#### Explanation

To determine how many bees are left in the hive, we need to calculate the value of

$$
32, 355 - 12, 247.
$$

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,7\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value from right to left, borrowing where needed.

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\, & \,\,\, & \,\,\, & \,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 4\end{aligned} & \,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 15\end{aligned} \\ & & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,5\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\, & \,\,\,7\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,8\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, there are $20,108$ bees left in the hive.
