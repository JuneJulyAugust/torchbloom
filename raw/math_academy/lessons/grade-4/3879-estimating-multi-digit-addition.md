## Lesson

### Introduction

Adding large numbers without using a calculator takes some effort. However, in some circumstances, it is sufficient to find an **estimate** for a particular sum. Computing an estimate is usually faster and easier than solving the original problem.

To estimate the answer to an addition problem, we round the numbers *first* and *then* add them.

To illustrate this, let's find an estimate for the value of

$$
382+567.
$$

Let's start by rounding both numbers to the nearest ten:

$$
\begin{aligned}382 & + & 567 \\ & & \\ 380 & + & 570\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we calculate the sum ${\color{blue}{380}} + {\color{blue}{570}}\mathbin{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & & \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
382+567 \approx 950.
$$

The symbol $\approx$ means "is approximately equal to."

The estimate we've obtained is pretty close to the actual value of the sum. In fact,

$$
382+567=949.
$$

In this example, we rounded to the nearest ten. In the next example, we'll estimate a sum by rounding both numbers to the nearest hundred.

### Example: Estimating Sums Containing Numbers Up to Three Digits

#### Question

There are currently $243$ trees in the park. Workers will plant $158$ more trees this week. By rounding each value to the nearest hundred, estimate how many trees will be in the park when the workers are finished.

#### Explanation

To estimate how many trees will be in the park when the workers are finished, we need to find an approximation for the sum

$$
243+158.
$$

First, we round both numbers to the nearest hundred:

$$
\begin{aligned}243 & + & 158 \\ & & \\ 200 & + & 200\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we calculate the sum ${\color{blue}{200}} + {\color{blue}{200}}\mathbin{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

So, we have

$$
243 + 158 \approx 400.
$$

Therefore, there will be approximately $400$ trees in the park when the workers are finished.

### Example: Estimating Sums Containing Numbers Up to Five Digits

#### Question

Estimate the sum $28,170 + 3,802$ by rounding each number to the nearest thousand.

#### Explanation

First, we round both numbers to the nearest thousand:

$$
\begin{aligned}28,170 & + & 3,802 \\ & & \\ 28,000 & + & 4,000\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we calculate the sum ${\color{blue}{28,000}} + {\color{blue}{4,000}}\mathbin{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & & & & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $28,170 + 3,802$ is approximately $32,000.$

### Example: Estimating Sums Containing Numbers Up to Seven Digits

#### Question

Estimate the sum $788,134 + 2,531,765$ by rounding each number to the nearest hundred thousand.

#### Explanation

First, we round both numbers to the nearest hundred- thousand:

$$
\begin{aligned}788,134 & + & 2,531,765 \\ & & \\ 800,000 & + & 2,500,000\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we calculate the sum ${\color{blue}{800,000}} + {\color{blue}{2,500,000}}\mathbin{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & & & & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $788,134 + 2,531,765$ is approximately $3,300,000.$
