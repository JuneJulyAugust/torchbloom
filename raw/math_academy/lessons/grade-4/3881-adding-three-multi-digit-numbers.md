## Lesson

### Introduction

We can use the standard algorithm to add multiple multi-digit whole numbers.

For example, let's use the standard algorithm to compute the following sum:

$$
801 + 523 + 198
$$

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we add the numbers in each place value (from right to left). However, this time, we need to sum three numbers in each place value, as well as any carried digits:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $801 + 523 + 198 = 1,522.$

### Example: Adding Three Numbers Up to Three Digits

#### Question

Lauren is listening to some songs while waiting for a train. The duration of the first song is $123$ seconds, the second is $182$ seconds, and the third is $157$ seconds. What is the total duration of all three songs?

#### Explanation

To find the total duration of all three tracks, we need to compute the sum

$$
123 + 182 + 157.
$$

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers using the standard algorithm:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

So, we have

$$
123 + 182 + 157 = 462.
$$

Therefore, the total duration of all three songs is $462$ seconds.

### Example: Adding Three Numbers Up to Five Digits

#### Question

What is $31,252 + 12,892 + 25,034?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers using the standard algorithm:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
31,252 + 12,892 + 25,034= 69,178.
$$

### Example: Adding Three Numbers Up to Seven Digits

#### Question

$2,078,503 + 148,450 + 1,885,047 =$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers using the standard algorithm:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
2,078,503 + 148,450 + 1,885,047 = 4,112,000.
$$
