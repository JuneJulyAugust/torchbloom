### Introduction

In this lesson, we will learn how to use the four operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division) to solve real-world multistep problems.

Let's take a look at the following situation:

*Chloe takes $18$ pieces of candy from a jar and another $15$ from a pack she bought. She plans to divide the candy equally between herself and two friends. How many pieces of candy will each person receive?*

We start by writing down the important information:

- Chloe took $18$ candy pieces from a jar.

- She has another $15$ pieces from a pack she bought.

- She wants to divide all the candy between $3$ people (herself, and two friends).

Let $N$ represent the number of pieces each person will receive. This is the number we want to find.

- Chloe took $18$ candy pieces from a jar, and she has $15$ more pieces from a pack she bought. Therefore, the total number of pieces equals

- The total number of candy pieces $({\color{blue}33})$ is then divided among $3$ people. Therefore, the total number of pieces each person will receive equals

According to our model, $N = 33\div 3 = 10+1 = 11.$

Therefore, each person will receive $11$ candy pieces.

### Example: Solving Problems Involving Division Followed by Addition or Subtraction

#### Question

Thelma divided $225\, \textrm{g}$ of jelly beans equally among four of her friends and herself. Then, from her share, she gave $25\, \textrm{g}$ to her little brother. How many grams of jelly beans did she keep for herself?

#### Explanation

Let's start by writing down the important information:

- Thelma had $225\,\textrm{g}$ of jelly beans.

- The jelly beans were equally divided between $5$ people ($4$ of Thelma's friends, and herself).

- From her share, she gave $25\, \textrm{g}$ to her brother.

Let $B$ represent the number of grams of jelly beans Thelma kept for herself. This is the number we want to find.

Now, we can deduce the following:

- Thelma divided the $225\, \textrm{g}$ of jelly beans equally among $5$ people, including herself. Therefore, each of them received $225 \div 5 = {\color{blue}45}\, \textrm{g}$ of jelly beans.

- The weight of jelly beans that Thelma kept for herself, $B$, equals the amount we calculated above $({\color{blue}45}\, \textrm{g})$ minus the amount she gave to her brother $({\color{red}25}\, \textrm{g}).$ Therefore, the total number of grams of jelly beans Thelma kept for herself is

Calculating the final difference, we get

$$
B = 45 - 25 = 20.
$$

Therefore, Thelma kept only $20\, \textrm{g}$ of jelly beans for herself.

Let's start by writing down the important information:

- There are $24$ packs of soda.

- Each pack contains $6$ soda cans.

- The fridge has $8$ shelves.

Let $S$ represent the total number of cans the clerk will put on each shelf. This is the number we want to find.

Now, we can deduce the following:

- There are $24$ packs of soda, and each pack contains $6$ cans. Therefore, the total number of soda cans is

- The total number of cans $({\color{blue}144})$ is divided equally among $8$ shelves. Therefore, the total number of cans of soda on each shelf is

Therefore, the clerk will put $18$ cans of soda on each shelf.

### Example: Solving Multistep Word Problems With Remainders
