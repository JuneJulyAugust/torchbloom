### Introduction

Any decimal containing *repeating* digits is rational. This means that every decimal containing repeating digits can be written as the ratio of two integers (i.e., as a fraction).

Let's see how to convert the decimal $0.3333\ldots$ to a fraction. We proceed in four steps:

- **Step 1:** We define our number as $x\mathbin{:}$ Notice that the *repeating digit* is $3.$

- **Step 2:** We multiply our number by $10$ to create a number with the same digits after the decimal point as $x\mathbin{:}$ So, we now have the following two numbers: These two numbers have the same repeating digit after the decimal point. In other words, they have the same "tail." This is crucial for the next step.

- **Step 3**: We subtract $x$ from $10x\mathbin{:}$ Notice that the tails canceled out, leaving us with the equation $9x=3$ that contains only integers.

- **Step 4**: Finally, we solve the equation for $x\mathbin{:}$

Therefore, we conclude that $0.3333 \ldots = \dfrac{1}{3}.$

### Example: Writing Decimals Containing One Repeating Digit as Fractions

#### Question

Write the repeating decimal $5.7777 \ldots$ as a fraction in its lowest terms.

#### Explanation

We proceed in four steps.

**** First, we define our number as $x\mathbin{:}$

$$


x=5.7777 \ldots


$$

Notice that the repeating digit is $7.$

**** We multiply our number by $10$ to create a number with the same digits after the decimal point as $x\mathbin{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}10×𝑥 & =10×5.7777… \\ 10𝑥 & =57.7777…\end{aligned}


$$

**** We subtract $x$ from $10x\mathbin{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}10𝑥 & =57.7777… \\ −\,𝑥 & =5.7777… \\ 9𝑥 & =52\end{aligned}


$$

**** Finally, we solve the equation for $x\mathbin{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}9𝑥 & =52 \\ \frac{9𝑥}{9} & =\frac{52}{9} \\ 𝑥 & =\frac{52}{9}\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, we conclude that $5.7777 \ldots = \dfrac{52}{9}.$

### Example: Writing Decimals Containing Multiple Repeating Digit as Fractions

#### Question

Express $0.\overline{23}$ as a fraction in its lowest terms.

#### Explanation

We're given the repeating decimal

$$


0.\overline{23} = 0.232323\ldots


$$

To express this as a fraction, we proceed in four steps.

**** First, we define our number as $x\mathbin{:}$

$$


x=0.2323 \ldots


$$

Notice that the repeating digits are $2$ and $3.$

**** Since we have two repeating digits, we multiply our number by $10^{2}=100$ to create a number with the same digits after the decimal point as $x\mathbin{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}100×𝑥 & =100×0.2323… \\ 100𝑥 & =23.2323…\end{aligned}


$$

****: We subtract $x$ from $100x\mathbin{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}100𝑥 & =23.2323… \\ −\,𝑥 & =0.2323… \\ 99𝑥 & =23\end{aligned}


$$

**** Finally, we solve the equation for $x\mathbin{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}99𝑥 & =23 \\ \frac{99𝑥}{99} & =\frac{23}{99} \\ 𝑥 & =\frac{23}{99}.\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, we conclude that $0.2323 \ldots = \dfrac{23}{99}.$

### Example: Writing Decimals With Repeating and Non-Repeating Digits as Fractions

#### Question

Express $0.26\overline{8}$ as a fraction in its lowest terms.

#### Explanation

We're given the repeating decimal

$$


0.26\overline{8} = 0.268888\ldots


$$

To express this as a fraction, we proceed in five steps.

**** We define our number as $x\mathbin{:}$

$$


x=0.268888 \ldots


$$

Notice that we have two non-repeating digits after the decimal point ($2$ and $6$) followed by the repeating digit $8.$

**** Since we have two ** digits, we multiply $x$ by $10^2 = 100$ to create a number containing only repeating digits after the decimal point. This gives the following:

$$


\begin{aligned}100𝑥 & =26.8888…\end{aligned}


$$

****. Since we have one ** digit, we multiply $100x$ by $10^{1}=10$ to create a number with the same digits after the decimal point as $100x\mathbin{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}10×100𝑥 & =10×26.8888… \\ 1000𝑥 & =268.8888…\end{aligned}


$$

**** We subtract $100x$ from $1000x\mathbin{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}1000𝑥 & =268.8888… \\ −\,100𝑥 & =26.8888… \\ 900𝑥 & =242\end{aligned}


$$

**** Finally, we solve the equation for $x\mathbin{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}900𝑥 & =242 \\ \frac{900𝑥}{900} & =\frac{242}{900} \\ 𝑥 & =\frac{242}{900} \\ 𝑥 & =\frac{121}{450}\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, we conclude that $0.268888 \ldots = \dfrac{121}{450}.$
