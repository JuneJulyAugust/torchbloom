## Lesson

### Introduction

We can use benchmarks to estimate decimal subtraction.

For example, suppose we want to estimate the following difference using benchmarks with decimal parts of $0.0$ or $0.5{:}$

$$
12.53 - 7.17
$$

We first round both numbers to the nearest benchmark:

$$
\begin{aligned}12.53 & − & 7.17 \\ & & \\ 12.5 & − & 7.0\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we calculate the difference:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $12.53 - 7.17$ is approximately $5.5,$ which we can write as follows:

$$
12.53 - 7.17 \approx 5.5
$$

### Example: Estimating Decimal Subtraction

#### Question

Estimate the difference $7.6 - 2.18$ using benchmarks with decimal parts of $0.0$ or $0.5.$

#### Explanation

First, we round both numbers to the nearest benchmark:

$$
\begin{aligned}7.6 & − & 2.18 \\ & & \\ 7.5 & − & 2.0\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we calculate the difference:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $7.6 - 2.18$ is approximately $5.5.$

### Example: Estimating Decimal Subtraction: Borrowing Once

#### Question

Estimate the difference $9.17 - 5.4$ using benchmarks with decimal parts of $0.0$ or $0.5.$

#### Explanation

First, we round both numbers to the nearest benchmark:

$$
\begin{aligned}9.17 & − & 5.4 \\ & & \\ 9.0 & − & 5.5\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we calculate the difference:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 8\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 10\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $9.17 - 5.4$ is approximately $3.5.$

### Example: Word Problems

#### Question

Three years ago, Rick was $4.43$ feet tall. He's now $5.11$ feet tall. Using benchmarks with decimal parts of $0.0,$ or $0.5$, estimate how much Rick grew during this period.

#### Explanation

To estimate how much Rick has grown during this period, we have to estimate the difference $5.11-4.43.$

First, we round both numbers to the nearest benchmark:

$$
\begin{aligned}5.11 & − & 4.43 \\ & & \\ 5.0 & − & 4.5\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we calculate the difference:

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 4\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 10\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, Rick grew by approximately $0.5$ feet during this period.
