## Lesson

### Introduction

We can use the standard algorithm to multiply a three-digit number by a one-digit number. In this case, we might need to carry a digit from the multiplication of the ones *and* tens.

For instance, let's find the value of

$$
143 \times 7.
$$

We start by writing the problem out as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \,10\,and\,2+10=1200000\end{aligned}
$$

First, we multiply the ones. We have $3\times 7 = {\color{blue}2}{\color{red}1}.$ We write $\color{red}1$ in the ones place and carry the ${\color{blue}2}.$

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,1\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}3×7=2100000000000000000 \\ Carry:\,2 \\ Write:\,1\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we multiply the tens as follows:

- We have $4\times 7= 28.$

- We add the $2$ carried from the previous multiplication to get $2+28 = {\color{blue}3}{\color{red}0}.$

- We write down $\color{red}0$ and carry ${\color{blue}3}.$

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}4×7=28\,and\,2+28=30 \\ Carry:\,3 \\ Write:\,0\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we multiply the hundreds as follows:

- We have $1\times 7 = 7.$

- We add the $3$ carried from the previous multiplication to get $3+7= 10.$

- Since this is the final multiplication, we write the entire result, ${\color{red}10}.$

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}1×7=7\,and\,3+7=10 \\ Carry:\,− \\ Write:\,10\end{aligned} \\ & & & \end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $143 \times 7 = 1,001.$

### Example: Multiplying a Three-Digit Number By a One-Digit Number: Finding Missing Digits

#### Question

From left to right, what are the missing digits in the following multiplication problem?

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,[math]\phantom{i}[/math]\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,[math]\phantom{i}[/math]\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

#### Explanation

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{5}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}9×6=54 \\ Carry:\,5 \\ Write:\,4\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{4}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{5}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}7×6=42\,and\,5+42=47 \\ Carry:\,4 \\ Write:\,7\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we multiply the hundreds:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{4}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{5}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,[math]\color{red}8[/math]\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}4×6=24\,and\,4+24=28 \\ Carry:\,− \\ Write:\,28\end{aligned} \\ & & & \end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the missing digits, from left-to-right, are $\bbox[3pt,Gainsboro]{\color{blue}8}$ and $\bbox[3pt,Gainsboro]{\color{blue}7}.$

### Example: Multiplying a Three-Digit Number By a One-Digit Number

#### Question

Find the value of $727 \times 6.$

#### Explanation

We write it this way:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \end{aligned}
$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{4}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}7×6=42 \\ Carry:\,4 \\ Write:\,2\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{1}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{4}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}2×6=12\,and\,4+12=16 \\ Carry:\,1 \\ Write:\,6\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we multiply the hundreds:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{1}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{4}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}7×6=42\,and\,1+42=43 \\ Carry:\,− \\ Write:\,43\end{aligned} \\ & & & \end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $727 \times 6 = 4 \, 362.$

### Example: Multiplying a Four-Digit Number By a One-Digit Number: Finding Missing Digits

#### Question

From left to right, what are the missing digits in the following multiplication problem?

$$
\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,[math]\phantom{i}[/math]\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,[math]\phantom{i}[/math]\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}
$$

#### Explanation

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{1}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}2×7=14 \\ Carry:\,1 \\ Write:\,4\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{1}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}5×7=35\,and\,1+35=36 \\ Carry:\,3 \\ Write:\,6\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we multiply the hundreds:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{5}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{1}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}8×7=56\,and\,3+56=59 \\ Carry:\,5 \\ Write:\,9\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we multiply the thousands:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{5}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{1}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,[math]\color{red}2[/math]\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}1×7=7\,and\,5+7=12 \\ Carry:\,− \\ Write:\,12\end{aligned} \\ & & & \end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the missing digits, from left-to-right, are $\bbox[3pt,Gainsboro]{\color{blue}2}$ and $\bbox[3pt,Gainsboro]{\color{blue}6}.$

### Example: Multiplying a Four-Digit Number By a One-Digit Number

#### Question

A bag of acorn seeds contains $2,162$ seeds in total. Mary purchases $4$ bags. How many seeds does she have in total?

#### Explanation

To calculate the total number of seeds, we need to multiply $2,162$ by $4$:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \,24\,and\,1+24=250000\end{aligned}
$$

First, we multiply the ones. Notice that there is no digit to carry.

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}2×4=80000000000000000 \\ Carry:\,− \\ Write:\,8\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we multiply the tens. This time there is a digit to carry, which we should place above the hundreds column.

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}6×4=240000000000000000 \\ Carry:\,2 \\ Write:\,4\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we multiply the hundreds:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}1×4=4\,and\,2+4=6 \\ Carry:\,− \\ Write:\,6\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we multiply the thousands:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}2×4=80000000000000000 \\ Carry:\,− \\ Write:\,8\end{aligned} \\ & & & \end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $2,162 \times 4 = 8, 648.$
