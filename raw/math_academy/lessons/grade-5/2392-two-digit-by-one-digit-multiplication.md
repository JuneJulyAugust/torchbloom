## Lesson

### Introduction

To multiply a two-digit number by a one-digit number, we can use the **standard algorithm**.

For example, to multiply $23$ by $3,$ we write the two-digit number above the one-digit number, lining up the place values:

$$
\begin{aligned}\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \,48+720=76800000000\end{aligned}
$$

First, we multiply the ones, placing the answer in the ones column under the line:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}3×3=90000000000000000 \\ Write:\,9\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we multiply by the value in the tens place, putting the answer in the tens column under the line:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}2×3=60000000000000000 \\ Write:\,6\end{aligned} \\ & & & \end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $23 \times 3 = 69.$

### Example: Two-Digit by One-Digit Multiplication

#### Question

What is $4$ multiplied by $21?$

#### Explanation

We write our multiplication this way:

$$
\begin{aligned}\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \,48+720=76800000000\end{aligned}
$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}1×4=40000000000000000 \\ Write:\,4\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we multiply the tens:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}2×4=80000000000000000 \\ Write:\,8\end{aligned} \\ & & & \end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $21 \times 4 = 84.$

### Multiplying Two-Digit Numbers By One-Digit Numbers With Carries

Sometimes when multiplying the ones, the result is two digits long.

In this case, we write down the digit in the ones place, and we **carry** the digit in the tens place. We then add the carried digit to the result of the multiplication of the tens.

For example, let's multiply $26$ by $6.$

$$
\begin{aligned}\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \,14\,and\,3+14=170000\end{aligned}
$$

First, we multiply the ones. We have $6\times6= {\color{blue}3}{\color{red}6}$, so we write the ones place value, $\color{red}6$, and carry the tens place value, ${\color{blue}3}\mathbin{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}6×6=3600000000000000000 \\ Carry:\,3 \\ Write:\,6\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we multiply the tens. We have $2\times 6 = 12$, to which we add the carry of ${\color{blue}3}\mathbin{:}$

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}2×6=12\,and\,3+12=15 \\ Carry:\,− \\ Write:\,15\end{aligned} \\ & & & \end{aligned}
$$

When we reach the tens multiplication, we do not carry anything. Instead, we write down the complete result.

Therefore, $26 \times 6 = 156.$

### Example: Two-Digit by One-Digit Multiplication With Carries

#### Question

Calculate $37\times 4.$

#### Explanation

We write it this way:

$$
\begin{aligned}\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}3×4=12\,and\,2+12=14\end{aligned} \\ & & \end{aligned}
$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}7×4=28\,and\,2+12=14 \\ Carry:\,2 \\ Write:\,8\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we multiply the tens:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}3×4=12\,and\,2+12=14 \\ Carry:\,− \\ Write:\,14\end{aligned} \\ & & & \end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $37 \times 4 = 148.$

### Example: Word Problems

#### Question

As part of a school assignment, each student in a class must interview $25$ people about their sports preferences. If there are $7$ students in the class, how many interviews will they need to conduct in total?

#### Explanation

To calculate the total number of interviews, we need to multiply $25$ by $7.$

$$
\begin{aligned}\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \,14\,and\,3+14=170000\end{aligned}
$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}5×7=3500000000000000000 \\ Carry:\,3 \\ Write:\,5\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we multiply the tens:

$$
\begin{aligned} & \begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{aligned} & \,\, & \begin{aligned}2×7=14\,and\,3+14=17 \\ Carry:\,− \\ Write:\,17\end{aligned} \\ & & & \end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, there will be a total of $175$ interviews.
