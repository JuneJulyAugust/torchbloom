#### Question

Which symbols could replace the empty box below to make the statement true?

4^2 \,\,\square\,\, 1^7

$$

1. $>$

2. $<$

3. $=$

#### Explanation

First, we calculate $4^2.$ We have

$$

2

$$

Then, for $1^7,$ we have

$$

\begin{aligned}1^{7}=\underset{{\color{red}7}  times}{\underset{}{1×1×1×1×1×1×1}}=1.\end{aligned}

Because $16 > 1,$ the correct statement must be

$$

### Example: Identifying the Correct Symbol When Comparing Fractional Exponents

#### Question

Which symbols could replace the empty box below to make the statement true?

$$

\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^2 \,\,\square\,\, \left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^1

$$

1. $>$

2. $<$

3. $=$

#### Explanation

First, we calculate $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^2.$ We have

$$

\begin{aligned}(\frac{1}{3})^{2} & =\underset{{\color{blue}2}  times}{\underset{}{\frac{1}{3}×\frac{1}{3}}} \\ & =\frac{1×1}{3×3} \\ & =\frac{1}{9}\,.\end{aligned}

$$

Then, for $\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^1,$ we have

$$

\begin{aligned}(\frac{1}{4})^{1}=\frac{1}{4}\,.\end{aligned}

$$

Because $\dfrac{1}{9} < \dfrac{1}{4},$ the correct statement must be

$$

\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^2 < \left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^1 \, .

$$

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."

### A Note on Exponent Convention

Let's work through the details of how to compute the following exponent:

$$

-5^2

$$

It's important to realize that this is shorthand for

$$

-(5)^2

$$

and *not* $(-5)^2.$

We start by calculating the positive exponent first, ignoring the negative sign:

$$

2

$$

Finally, we replace the negative sign. Therefore, we conclude that

$$

- 5^2 = -25.

$$

3. $=$

#### Explanation

**** Remember that,

- $-2^3$ is shorthand for $-\!\left(2^3\right),$

- similarly, $-3^2$ is shorthand for $-\!\left(3^2\right).$

First, we calculate $-2^3.$ We ignore the negative sign and calculate the positive exponent first:

$$

3

$$

Therefore, $- 2^3 = -8.$

Now, to compute $- 3^2,$ we again ignore the negative sign and calculate the positive exponent first:

$$

\begin{aligned}3^{2}=\underset{{\color{red}2}  times}{\underset{}{3×3}}=9\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $- 3^2 = -9.$

Finally, let's look at the number line to compare $-8$ and $-9.$
