## Lesson

### Introduction

Decimal subtraction works in a similar way to whole number subtraction.

As an example, let's find the value of $0.8 - 0.5.$

First, we write one number above the other, lining up the decimal points and placing a decimal point below the others in our answer:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \end{aligned}



$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore:

$$



0.8 - 0.5= 0.3



$$

This is just like $8-5=3,$ but with the decimal point in a different position.

### Example: Subtracting Decimals

#### Question

Find the difference $8.17 - 0.14.$

#### Explanation

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & & \end{aligned}



$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore:

$$



8.17 - 0.14 = 8.03



$$

### Example: Subtracting Decimals: Borrowing Once

#### Question

What is $5.7 - 3.9?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \end{aligned}



$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 4\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 17\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore:

$$



5.7 - 3.9 = 1.8



$$

### Example: Subtracting Decimals: Borrowing More Than Once

#### Question

Find the value of $7.362 - 0.634.$

#### Explanation

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & & & \end{aligned}



$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 6\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 13\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 5\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 12\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore:

$$



7.362 - 0.634 = 6.728



$$

### Example: Subtracting Decimals: Word Problems

#### Question

A consignment of strawberries in a dispatch van weighs $208.5$ pounds. The van must deliver the load to two stores. If it unloads $124.7$ pounds of those strawberries at the first store, how much will it unload at the second store?

#### Explanation

To find out how much of the strawberries the van will unload at the second store, we need to calculate

$$



208.5-124.7 \, .



$$

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \end{aligned}



$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 1\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 10\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 7\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 15\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, the van will unload $83.8$ pounds of strawberries at the second store.
