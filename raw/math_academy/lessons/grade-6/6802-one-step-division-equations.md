### Introduction

A **one-step division equation** is an equation like

$$



\dfrac{x}{5}=7,



$$

where the variable is divided by a number.

To solve a one-step division equation, we isolate the variable by reversing the operation that is applied to it.

For example, in the equation above, the variable $x$ is *divided* by $5.$ The opposite of dividing by $5$ is *multiplying* by $5.$

So, we multiply both sides of the equation by $5{:}$

$$



\begin{aligned}\frac{𝑥}{5} & =7 \\ \frac{𝑥}{5}×5 & =7×5 \\ 𝑥 & =35.\end{aligned}



$$

This isolates the variable, so we have found the solution.

**Note**: We can see how multiplying by the denominator removes it from the left-hand side by swapping denominators and simplifying:

$$



\begin{aligned}\frac{𝑥}{5}×5 & = \\ \frac{𝑥}{1}×\frac{5}{5} & = \\ 𝑥×1 & = \\ 𝑥 & \end{aligned}



$$

Recall that if the variable appears on the right-hand side $\Big($e.g., $7=\dfrac{x}5\Big),$ you may first swap the left and right sides of the equation to get $\dfrac{x}5=7.$

#### Question

#### Explanation

To solve a linear equation, we isolate the variable by reversing the operations applied to it, performing each inverse operation on both sides of the equation step by step.

In this case, the variable $p$ is ** by $8.$ To isolate $p,$ we can perform the ** operation, which is ** by $8.$ So, we multiply both sides of the equation by $8{:}$

$$



\begin{aligned}\frac{𝑝}{8} & =15 \\ \frac{𝑝}{8}×8 & =15×8 \\ 𝑝 & =120\end{aligned}



$$

### Example: Solving One-Step Division Equations With Integers: Variable on the Right-Hand Side

#### Question

If $5=\dfrac{x}{9},$ then $x=$

#### Explanation

To solve a linear equation, we isolate the variable by reversing the operations applied to it, performing each inverse operation on both sides of the equation step by step.

First, let's swap the left and right-hand sides so that the variable is on the left-hand side.

$$



\dfrac{x}9 = 5



$$

In this case, the variable $x$ is ** by $9.$ To isolate $x,$ we can perform the ** operation, which is ** by $9.$ So, we multiply both sides of the equation by $9{:}$

$$



\begin{aligned}\frac{𝑥}{9} & =5 \\ \frac{𝑥}{9}×9 & =5×9 \\ 𝑥 & =45\end{aligned}



$$

### Example: Solving One-Step Division Equations With Fractions on Both Sides

#### Question

If $\dfrac{k}{10}=\dfrac25,$ then $k=$

#### Explanation

To solve a linear equation, we isolate the variable by reversing the operations applied to it, performing each inverse operation on both sides of the equation step by step.

In this case, the variable $k$ is ** by $10.$ To isolate $k,$ we can perform the ** operation, which is ** by $10.$ So, we multiply both sides of the equation by $10{:}$

$$



\begin{aligned}\frac{𝑘}{10} & =\frac{2}{5} \\ \frac{𝑘}{10}×10 & =\frac{2}{5}×10 \\ 𝑘 & =\frac{20}{5} \\ 𝑘 & =4\end{aligned}



$$
