## Lesson

### Introduction

Suppose we want to compute the sum of two numbers with different numbers of decimal places, such as

$$



19+5.6.



$$

Here, there are no decimal places in $19,$ but there is $\color{blue}1$ decimal place in $5.6.$

In order to add the two numbers, we need the two numbers to have the same number of decimal places. We can achieve this by attaching an extra zero behind the decimal place in the first number, so that it has $\color{blue}1$ decimal place:

$$



1



$$

Remember that whenever we attach an extra zero behind the decimal place, the number remains unchanged. So the number $19.0$ is equivalent to the original number $19.$

Now the addition problem reads

$$



19.0 + 5.6,



$$

and we can add the decimal numbers using the usual method. First, we line up the decimal points:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \end{aligned}



$$

Then, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 1\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & & & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, $19 + 5.6 = 24.6.$

### Example: Adding Decimals and Whole Numbers

#### Question

What is the value of $6 + 16.4?$

#### Explanation

There are no decimal places in $6$ and there is $\color{blue}1$ decimal place in $16.4.$

To add the numbers, they both need the same number of decimals. Hence, we rewrite the first number so that it has $\color{blue}1$ decimal zero:

$$



1



$$

Next, we line up the decimal points:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \end{aligned}



$$

Finally, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 1\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, $6 + 16.4 = 22.4.$

### Example: Adding Numbers With One and Two Decimal Places

#### Question

What is $15.4 + 20.79?$

#### Explanation

There is $\color{blue}1$ decimal place in $15.4$ and there are $\color{blue}2$ decimal places in $20.79.$

In order to add the two numbers, we need them to have the same number of decimal places. So, we rewrite the first number so that it has $\color{blue}2$ decimal places:

$$



15.4=15.4{\color{red}0}



$$

Next, we line up the decimal points:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & & \end{aligned}



$$

Finally, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 1\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & & & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, $15.4 + 20.79 = 36.19.$

### Example: Adding Numbers With Two and Three Decimal Places

#### Question

What is the value of $32.54 + 16.854?$

#### Explanation

There are $\color{blue}2$ decimal places in $32.54$ and there are $\color{blue}3$ decimal places in $16.854.$

To add the two numbers, we need them to have the same number of decimal places. So, we rewrite the second number so that it has $\color{blue}3$ decimal places:

$$



32.54=32.54{\color{red}0}



$$

Next, we line up the decimal points:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & & & \end{aligned}



$$

Finally, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 1\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & & & & \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, $32.54 + 16.854 = 49.394.$

### Decimal Addition With Multiple Decimal Place Differences

Sometimes, we will need to place more than one zero to the right of a decimal point.

For instance, to find the value of $78.9 + 45.962,$ notice that there is $\color{blue}1$ decimal place in $78.9$ and there are $\color{blue}3$ decimal places in $45.962.$

To add the two numbers, we need them to have the same number of decimal places. So, we rewrite the first number so that it has $\color{blue}3$ decimal places:

$$



78.9=78.9{\color{red}00}



$$

Next, we line up the decimal points:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & & & \end{aligned}



$$

Finally, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$



\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 1\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\begin{aligned} \\ 1\end{aligned}\,\,\,\, & & & & \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, $78.9 + 45.962 = 124.862.$
