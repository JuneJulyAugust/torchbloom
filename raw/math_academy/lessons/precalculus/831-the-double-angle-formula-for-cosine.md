## Lesson

### Introduction

Recall the sum formula for cosine:

$$


\cos(x+y) = \cos x \cos y - \sin x \sin y


$$

Substituting $y={\color{blue}x}$ into the above gives

$$


\begin{aligned}cos⁡(𝑥+𝑥) & =cos⁡𝑥cos⁡𝑥−sin⁡𝑥sin⁡𝑥,\end{aligned}


$$

which simplifies to

$$


\cos 2x = \cos^2 {x} - \sin^2 {x}.


$$

This is called the **double-angle formula for cosine**.

### Example: Using the Double-Angle Formula to Simplify Expressions

#### Question

Express $\cos^2{\left(\dfrac{\pi}{24}\right)} - \sin^2{\left(\dfrac{\pi}{24}\right)}$ as a single trigonometric ratio.

#### Explanation

First, let's recall the double-angle formula for cosine:

$$


\cos^2 x - \sin^2 x = \cos 2x


$$

Substituting $x={\dfrac{\pi}{24}}$ into the above, we get

$$


\begin{aligned}cos^{2}⁡(\frac{𝜋}{24})−sin^{2}⁡(\frac{𝜋}{24}) & =cos⁡(2⋅\frac{𝜋}{24}) \\ & =cos⁡(\frac{𝜋}{12}).\end{aligned}


$$

### Alternative Forms of the Double-Angle Formula for Cosine

It's possible to write the double angle formula for cosine in two alternative forms.

From the Pythagorean identity $\sin^2{x}+\cos^2{x}=1,$ we obtain $\cos^2{x}=1-\sin^2{x}.$ Substituting this into the double-angle formula for cosine, we get

$$


\begin{aligned}cos⁡2𝑥 & =cos^{2}⁡𝑥−sin^{2}⁡𝑥 \\ & =(1−sin^{2}⁡𝑥)−sin^{2}⁡𝑥 \\ & =1−2sin^{2}⁡𝑥.\end{aligned}


$$

This gives an alternative form of the double-angle formula for cosine. We'll restate it below:

$$


\cos{2x} =1 - 2\sin^2{x}


$$

Similarly, from the same Pythagorean identity, we obtain that $\sin^2{x}=1-\cos^2{x}.$ Substituting into the double-angle formula for cosine, we get

$$


\begin{aligned}cos⁡2𝑥 & =cos^{2}⁡𝑥−sin^{2}⁡𝑥 \\ & =cos^{2}⁡𝑥−(1−cos^{2}⁡𝑥) \\ & =2cos^{2}⁡𝑥−1.\end{aligned}


$$

This is another alternative form of the double-angle formula for cosine:

$$


\cos{2x} =2\cos^2{x}-1


$$

### Example: Using Alternative Forms of Double-Angle Formula to Simplify Expressions

#### Question

Express $2\cos^2{\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)}-1$ as a single trigonometric ratio.

#### Explanation

First, let's recall the double-angle formula for cosine:

$$


2 \cos^2 x - 1 = \cos 2x


$$

Substituting $x={\dfrac{\pi}{3}}$ into the above, we get

$$


\begin{aligned}2cos^{2}⁡(\frac{𝜋}{3})−1 & =cos⁡(2⋅\frac{𝜋}{3}) \\ & =cos⁡(\frac{2𝜋}{3}).\end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Finding Exact Values of Trigonometric Expressions Using the Double-Angle Formula

#### Question

Without using a calculator, find the exact value of $\cos^2\left(22.5^\circ\right) - \sin^2\left(22.5^\circ\right).$

#### Explanation

First, let's recall the double-angle formula for cosine:

$$


\cos^2 x - \sin^2 x = \cos 2x


$$

Substituting $x={22.5^\circ}$ into the above, we get

$$


\begin{aligned}cos^{2}⁡(22.5^{∘})−sin^{2}⁡(22.5^{∘}) & =cos⁡(2⋅22.5^{∘}) \\ & =cos⁡45^{∘} \\ & =\frac{\sqrt{√2}}{2}.\end{aligned}


$$
