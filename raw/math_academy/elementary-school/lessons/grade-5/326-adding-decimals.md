## Lesson

### Introduction

Adding decimals is no harder than adding whole numbers.

For example, let's find $0.4 + 2.6.$

First, we write one number above the other, lining up the decimal points and placing a decimal point below the others in our answer:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers. After each addition, we write the ones place and carry the tens place of the result.

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ 1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & & \\ & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $0.4 + 2.6 = 3.$

### Example: Adding Two-Digit and Three-Digit Decimals

#### Question

What is $0.94 + 8.32?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & & \end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ 1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & & & \\ & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $0.94 + 8.32 = 9.26.$

### Example: Adding Decimals Containing Different Numbers of Whole Digits

#### Question

What is $2.43 + 50.41?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & & \end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $2.43 + 50.41 = 52.84.$

### Example: Adding Four-Digit, Five-Digit and Six-Digit Decimals

#### Question

What is $982.97 + 306.74?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & & \end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & & & \,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ 1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ 1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $1,289.71.$

### Example: Word Problems

#### Question

Brandon stirs $4.76$ liters of milk with $2.82$ liters of water in a pot. How much liquid is in the pot?

#### Explanation

To find how much liquid is in the pot, we need to calculate $4.76 + 2.82.$

First, we line up the decimal points:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & & \end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by adding the two numbers just as we would with whole numbers:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ 1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & & & \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,.\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, there are $7.58$ liters of liquid in the pot.
