## Lesson

### Introduction

Mixed numbers are a common occurrence in everyday life. Therefore, we can apply our knowledge of adding and subtracting mixed numbers to real-world situations.

For example, suppose Daniela is sewing a costume. She needs $2\,\dfrac{1}{6}$ yards of gold fabric and $1\,\dfrac{1}{3}$ yards of purple fabric. How many yards of fabric does she need to make her costume?

To find the total yards of fabric that Daniela needs, we would need to add $2\,\dfrac{1}{6}$ (the number of yards of gold fabric) to $1\,\dfrac{1}{3}$ (the number of yards of purple fabric):

$$

2\,\dfrac{1}{6} + 1\,\dfrac{1}{3}

$$

To add two mixed numbers, we add whole numbers to whole numbers and fractions to fractions. Adding the whole numbers, we get $2+1 = {\color{red}{3}}.$ Now, we add the fractions:

$$

\dfrac{1}{6} + \dfrac{1}{3}

$$

To add two fractions with unlike denominators, we need to express each fraction as an equivalent fraction with a common denominator. In this question, we can make a common denominator of $6.$

- The fraction $\dfrac{1}{6}$ already has a denominator of $6.$

- To put $\dfrac{1}{3}$ over a denominator of $6,$ we multiply the numerator and denominator by $2$:

We can now add the fractions. We keep the denominator the same and we add the numerators:

$$

\begin{aligned}\frac{1}{6}+\frac{2}{6}=\frac{3}{6}\end{aligned}

$$

We can simplify this fraction by dividing the numerator and denominator by $3\mathbin{:}$

$$

\dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{3\div 3}{6\div 3} = {\color{blue}{\dfrac12}}

$$

Adding, we get

$$

2\,\dfrac{1}{6} + 1\,\dfrac{1}{3} = {\color{red}{3}} + {\color{blue}{\dfrac12}} = 3\,\dfrac{1}{2}.

$$

Therefore, Daniela needs $3\,\dfrac{1}{2}$ yards of fabric to make her costume.

### Example: Adding Mixed Numbers With Unlike Denominators

#### Question

Nadia, a construction worker, cuts pieces of wood for her colleagues. She cuts $1\,\dfrac{3}{4}\,\text{yd}$ of wood for Louis and $2\,\dfrac{7}{10}\,\text{yd}$ of wood for Diana. What is the total length of wood she gave to her two colleagues?

#### Explanation

To find the length of wood she gave to Louis and Diana, we need to calculate the sum $1\,\dfrac{3}{4} + 2\,\dfrac{7}{10}.$

To add two mixed numbers, we add whole numbers to whole numbers and fractions to fractions. Adding the whole numbers, we get $1+2 = {\color{red}{3}}.$ Now, we add the fractions:

$$

\dfrac{3}{4} + \dfrac{7}{10}

$$

To add two fractions with unlike denominators, we need to express each fraction as an equivalent fraction with a common denominator. In this question, we can make a common denominator of $20.$

- To put $\dfrac{3}{4}$ over a denominator of $20,$ we multiply the numerator and denominator by $5$:

- To put $\dfrac{7}{10}$ over a denominator of $20,$ we multiply the numerator and denominator by $2$:

We can now add the fractions. We keep the denominator the same and we add the numerators:

$$

\begin{aligned}\frac{15}{20}+\frac{14}{20}=\frac{29}{20}\end{aligned}

$$

We can convert the fraction $\dfrac{29}{20}$ to a mixed number:

$$

29 \div 20 = 1\,\text{R}\,9 = {\color{blue}{1\,\dfrac 9{20}}}

$$

Adding, we get

$$

1\,\dfrac{3}{4} + 2\,\dfrac{7}{10} = {\color{red}{3}} + {\color{blue}{1\,\dfrac 9{20}}} = 4\,\dfrac{9}{20}.

$$

Therefore, in total, Nadia cut $4\,\dfrac{9}{20}\,\text{yd}$ of wood.

### Example: Subtracting Mixed Numbers With Unlike Denominators

#### Question

Christian used $1\dfrac{3}{4}\,\text{L}$ of milk to make pancakes. Before making the pancakes, he had $2\,\dfrac{6}{7}\,\text{L}$ of milk. How much milk does he have now?

#### Explanation

To determine the amount of milk Christian has left, we need to calculate the difference $2\,\dfrac{6}{7} - 1\dfrac{3}{4}.$

To subtract two mixed numbers, we subtract whole numbers from whole numbers and fractions from fractions. Subtracting the whole numbers, we get $2-1 = {\color{red}{1}}.$ Now we subtract the fractions:

$$

\dfrac{6}{7} - \dfrac 3 4

$$

Let's look at the denominators. The least common denominator of $4$ and $7$ is $28.$

- To put $\dfrac 6 7$ over a denominator of $28,$ we multiply the numerator and denominator by $4$:

- To put $\dfrac 3 4$ over a denominator of $28,$ we multiply the numerator and denominator by $7$:

We can now subtract the fractions. We keep the denominator the same and we subtract the numerators:

$$

\begin{aligned}\frac{6}{7}−\frac{3}{4}=\frac{24}{28}−\frac{21}{28}=\frac{3}{28}\end{aligned}

$$

Subtracting, we get

$$

2\,\dfrac{6}{7} - \dfrac{3}{4} = {\color{red}{1}}\,{\color{blue}\dfrac{3}{28}}.

$$

So, Christian has $1\dfrac{3}{28}\,\text{L}$ of milk remaining.
