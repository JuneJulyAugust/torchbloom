## Lesson

### Introduction

We can use the standard algorithm to multiply two-digit numbers.

To illustrate, let's use the standard algorithm to compute $12\times 13.$

Recall that ${\color{blue}1}{\color{red}3}$ in expanded form is ${\color{blue}10} + {\color{red}3}.$ The idea is to first multiply $12$ by ${\color{red}3},$ then by ${\color{blue}10},$ and add the results.

We write one number above the other as follows:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{matrix} & \,\, & 36+120=156\end{aligned}

$$

First, we multiply the top number ($12$) by the value in the ones place of the second number ($\boxed{3}$), writing the result under the line:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}12×3=36000\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply $12$ by $\boxed{1}0.$ Let's do this in steps:

**Step 1**: Any number multiplied by $10$ always gives a zero at the end. Therefore, we place a zero in the ones column underneath our first result.

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}12×10=1200\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

**Step 2**: Now, we work out $\boxed{2}\times \boxed{1}.$ We place the answer next to our zero:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}2×1=20\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

**Step 3**: Next, we we work out $\boxed{1}\times \boxed{1}$:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}1×1=10\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Finally, we add the results:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}36+120=156\end{matrix} \\ & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $12 \times 13 = 156.$

### Example: Multiplying a Two-Digit Number by a Two-Digit Number

#### Question

What is $23$ multiplied by $22?$

#### Explanation

We write it this way:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{matrix} & \,\, & 46+460=506\end{aligned}

$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}23×2=46000\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}23×20=4600\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Finally, we add the results:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}46+460=506\end{matrix} \\ & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $23 \times 22 = 506.$

### Multiplying Two-Digit Numbers by Two-Digit Numbers with Carries

Just like when multiplying a number by a one-digit number, we will sometimes have carries. Let's see how to deal with this by multiplying $24$ by $32.$

We write it this way:

$$

\begin{aligned}\,\, & \begin{matrix} & & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{matrix} & \,\, & 48+720=768\end{aligned}

$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$

\begin{aligned}\,\, & \begin{matrix} & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}0 \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}24×2=48000\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

On this occasion, there were no carries when multiplying the ones, and so the carry box $0$ remains empty.

Next, we multiply the tens. Let's do this in steps.

**Step 1**: We start by writing a zero:

$$

\begin{aligned}\,\, & \begin{matrix} & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}0 \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}24×30=7200\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

**Step 2**: We now work out $\boxed{4}\times \boxed{3} = {\color{blue}1}{\color{red}2}.$ We carry the ${\color{blue}1}$ and write down $\color{red}2$:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}4×3=12 \\ Carry:\,1 \\ Write:\,2\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

**Step 3**: We multiply $\boxed{2}\times \boxed{3} = 6,$ and we add the carry from the previous multiplication to make $6+{\color{blue}{1}} = {\color{red}{7}}.$

$$

\begin{aligned}\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \begin{matrix} & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}2×3=6\,and\,6+1=7 \\ Carry:\,− \\ Write:\,7\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Finally, we add the results:

$$

\begin{aligned}\, & \begin{matrix} & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}48+720=768\end{matrix} \\ & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $24 \times 32 = 768.$

### Example: Multiplying a Two-Digit Number by a Two-Digit Number With Carried Digits

#### Question

Calculate $47 \times 35.$

#### Explanation

As seen before, we start by writing one number on top of the other:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{matrix} & \,\, & 00000000000000000\end{aligned}

$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ \color{blue}3\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}47×5=23500000\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}2 \\ \color{blue}3\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}47×30=1,41000\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Finally, we add the results:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}2 \\ \color{blue}3\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}235+1410=1,645\end{matrix} \\ & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $47 \times 35 = 1, 645.$

### Example: Multiplying a Two-Digit Number by a Two-Digit Number: Word Problems

#### Question

Jessica runs $12$ kilometers every morning. How many kilometers does Jessica run in $28$ days?

#### Explanation

To calculate the total number of kilometers that Jessica runs in $28$ days, we need to multiply $12$ by $28.$

So, we write it this way:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{matrix} & \,\, & 12×20=240\end{aligned}

$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ \color{blue}1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}12×8=96000\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}12×20=2400\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Finally, we add the results:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}96+240=336\end{matrix} \\ & & & \end{aligned}

$$

So, $12 \times 28 = 336.$

Therefore, Jessica runs a total of $336$ kilometers in $28$ days.
