## Lesson

### Introduction

In this lesson, we'll get practice at multiplying five-digit and six-digit numbers by one-digit numbers using the standard algorithm.

As before, we multiply each place value by the one-digit number from right to left.

Without further ado, let's dive in!

### Example: Multiplying Larger Numbers By One-Digit Numbers: Finding Missing Digits

#### Question

From left to right, what are the missing digits in the following multiplication problem?

$$

\begin{aligned}\begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{5}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\phantom{4}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\phantom{7}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\phantom{0}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

#### Explanation

First, we multiply the ones:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}6×6=36 \\ Carry:\,3 \\ Write:\,6\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}0×6=0\,and\,3+0=3 \\ Carry:\,0 \\ Write:\,3\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the hundreds:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}0×6=0\,and\,0+0=0 \\ Carry:\,0 \\ Write:\,0\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the thousands:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{5}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}9×6=54\,and\,0+54=54 \\ Carry:\,5 \\ Write:\,4\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Finally, we multiply the tens of thousands:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{5}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\color{red}4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\color{red}7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}7×6=42\,and\,5+42=47 \\ Carry:\,− \\ Write:\,47\end{matrix} \\ & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, the missing digits, from left-to-right, are $\boxed{\color{blue}4},\boxed{\color{blue}7},$ and $\boxed{\color{blue}0}.$

### Example: Multiplying Larger Numbers By One-Digit Numbers

#### Question

Calculate the value of $43,532 \times 7.$

#### Explanation

We write it this way:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \,24\,and\,1+24=2500000\end{aligned}

$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{1}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}2×7=1400000000000000000 \\ Carry:\,1 \\ Write:\,4\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{1}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}3×7=21\,and\,1+21=22 \\ Carry:\,2 \\ Write:\,2\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the hundreds:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{1}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}5×7=35\,and\,2+35=37 \\ Carry:\,3 \\ Write:\,7\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the thousands:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{1}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}3×7=21\,and\,3+21=24 \\ Carry:\,2 \\ Write:\,4\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Finally, we multiply the ten thousands:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{1}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}4×7=28\,and\,2+28=30 \\ Carry:\,− \\ Write:\,30\end{matrix} \\ & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $43,532 \times 7 = 304, 724.$

### Example: Word Problems

#### Question

A factory uses $836\,802$ kilowatt-hours of electricity each quarter. How much electricity will it use in a year, if a year contains $4$ quarters?

#### Explanation

To calculate the factory's energy consumption over one year, we need to multiply $836\,802$ by $4.$

We write it this way:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \end{aligned}

$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}2×4=8 \\ Carry:\,0 \\ Write:\,8\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}0×4=0\,and\,0+0=0 \\ Carry:\,0 \\ Write:\,0\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the hundreds:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}8×4=32\,and\,0+32=32 \\ Carry:\,3 \\ Write:\,2\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the thousands:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}6×4=24\,and\,3+24=27 \\ Carry:\,2 \\ Write:\,7\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the ten thousands:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{1}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}3×4=12\,and\,2+12=14 \\ Carry:\,1 \\ Write:\,4\end{matrix} \\ & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Finally, we multiply the hundred thousands:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & \,\,\,\,\underset{1}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{2}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{3}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\underset{0}{}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & & & & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}8×4=32\,and\,1+32=33 \\ Carry:\,− \\ Write:\,33\end{matrix} \\ & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $836\,802 \times 4 = 3 \, 347 \, 208.$

Therefore, the factory's total energy consumption in a year is $3\,347\,208$ kilowatt-hours.
