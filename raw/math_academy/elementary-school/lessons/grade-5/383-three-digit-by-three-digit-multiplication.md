### Introduction

The standard algorithm for multiplication works in the same way when multiplying three-digit numbers by three-digit numbers.

To demonstrate, let's compute $237 \times 142.$ We start by writing it in the usual way:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & & & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & & & \end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}474+9,480+23,700=\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ \color{blue}1\end{matrix}\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,2\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}237×2=4740000000,\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the tens. Remember that when multiplying the tens, the result will always end in a zero.

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}2 \\ \color{blue}1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}237×40=9,48000000\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the hundreds. Since a hundred ($100$) has two zeros at the end, the result will end in *two* zeros! So, we write these first:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}2 \\ \color{blue}1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}237×100=23,700000\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Then, we proceed to multiply the rest of the hundreds as usual.

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}2 \\ \color{blue}1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}237×100=23,700000\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Finally, we add the results:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}2 \\ \color{blue}1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}474+9,480+23,700= \\ \,=33,654\end{matrix} \\ & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $237 \times 142 = 33, 654.$

### Example: Three-Digit by Three-Digit Multiplication: Find the Missing Digits

#### Question

What are the missing digits in the following multiplication problem?

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\phantom{i}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\phantom{i}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\phantom{i}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \end{aligned}

$$

#### Explanation

We write it this way:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & & & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & & & \end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}180+1,800+90,000=\,\,\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}180×1=18000000,,\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}180×10=1,800000,,\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the hundreds:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}4 \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}180×500=90,0000,,\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Finally, we add the results:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}4 \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}180+1,800+90,000= \\ \,=91,980\end{matrix} \\ & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, the missing digits are $1$, $9$ and $8.$

### Example: Three-Digit by Three-Digit Multiplication

#### Question

Find the value of $435 \times 435.$

#### Explanation

We write it this way:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & & & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & & & \end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}2,175+13,050+174,000=\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ \color{blue}1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ \color{blue}2\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}435×5=2,175000000000,\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}2\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}435×30=13,05000000000,\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the hundreds:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}2 \\ \color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}2\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}435×400=174,000000000,\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Finally, we add the results:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}1\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}2 \\ \color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}2\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}2,175+13,050+174,000= \\ \,=189,225\end{matrix} \\ & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $435 \times 435 = 189, 225.$

### Example: Three-Digit by Three-Digit Multiplication: Word Problem

#### Question

Richard has $504$ boxes stored in a warehouse. Each box contains $240$ plates. How many plates in total are there in the warehouse?

#### Explanation

To find the total number of plates stored in the warehouse, we need to calculate $504 \times 240.$

We write it this way:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & & & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & & & \end{matrix} & \,\, & 0+20,160+100,800=\end{aligned}

$$

First, we multiply the ones:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}504×0=00000000000\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the tens:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}504×40=20,1600000\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we multiply the hundreds:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}504×200=100,80000\end{matrix}\end{aligned}

$$

Finally, we add the results:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \begin{matrix} & & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\begin{matrix}\color{blue}\phantom{0} \\ \color{blue}1 \\ \color{blue}\phantom{0}\end{matrix}\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,×\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{matrix} & \,\, & \begin{matrix}0+20,160+100,800= \\ \,=120,960\end{matrix} \\ & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Hence, $504 \times 240 = 120, 960.$

Therefore, Richard has $120,960$ plates in the warehouse.
