### Introduction

Whenever we write a decimal as a mixed number, our mixed number will have

- a **whole number part** corresponding to the decimal's whole number part, and

- a **fractional part** corresponding to the decimal part.

For example, let's write the following decimal as a mixed number:

$$

{\color{blue}{5.}}{\color{red}{7}}

$$

To express this decimal as a mixed number, we look at its whole number and decimal parts:

- The whole number part is ${\color{blue}5}.$

- The decimal part has $1$ decimal place. Therefore, we can write it as a decimal fraction as follows:

Therefore, we express our decimal as a mixed number in the following way:

$$

$$
