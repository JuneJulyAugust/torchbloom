### Introduction

Let's take a look at the following addition problem:

$$

132 + 46

$$

In this case, we're adding a two-digit number to a three-digit number. To do this, we use the standard algorithm.

The first step is to write the numbers in the usual way, where ones are aligned over ones, tens over tens, and hundreds over hundreds:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left) starting from the ones:

**Step 1.** Adding the ones, we get ${\color{blue}2} + {\color{blue}6} = {\color{blue}8}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**Step 2.** Adding the tens, we get ${\color{blue}3} + {\color{blue}4} = {\color{blue}7}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**Step 3.** Notice that $46$ has no hundred digit. Therefore, we place a $\color{red}0$ in its hundreds place:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Then, adding the hundreds, we get ${\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}0} = {\color{blue}1}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $132 + 46 = 178.$

### Example: Adding Three-Digit and Two-Digit Whole Numbers Without Carrying

#### Question

Tom and Jerry are running a race that takes place over several days. Tom runs a total of $122$ kilometers, while Jerry runs $36$ kilometers more than Tom. How many kilometers did Jerry run?

#### Explanation

To find the number of kilometers Jerry ran, we need to calculate the value of

$$

122 + 36.

$$

First, we line up our numbers (** over **, ** over **, ** over **):

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**** Adding the ones, ${\color{blue}2} + {\color{blue}6} = {\color{blue}8}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**** Adding the tens, ${\color{blue}2} + {\color{blue}3} = {\color{blue}5}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**** Adding the hundreds, ${\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}0} = {\color{blue}1}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, Jerry ran $158$ kilometers.

### Adding With Carrying

Now, let's calculate the following sum:

$$

119 + 71

$$

To use the standard algorithm, we first line up our numbers (*ones* over *ones*, *tens* over *tens*, *hundreds* over *hundreds*):

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** Adding the ones, we get ${\color{blue}9} + {\color{blue}1} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}0}.$ However, we can't write ${\color{red}1}{\color{blue}0}$ in the ones column. So, we write ${\color{blue}0}$ below the *ones* and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the *tens*:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**Step 2.** Adding the tens, we get ${\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}7} = {\color{blue}9}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**Step 3.** Adding the hundreds, we get ${\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}0} = {\color{blue}1}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $119 + 71 = 190.$

### Example: Adding Three-Digit and Two-Digit Whole Numbers With Carrying

#### Question

What is $51 + 267?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers (** over **, ** over **, ** over **):

$$

\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**** Adding the ones, ${\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}7} = {\color{blue}8}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**** Adding the tens, ${\color{blue}5} + {\color{blue}6} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}1}.$ We write ${\color{blue}1}$ below the ** and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the **:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**** Adding the hundreds, ${\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}0} + {\color{blue}2} = {\color{blue}3}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & & \\ & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $51 + 267 = 318.$

### Example: Adding Three-Digit and Two-Digit Whole Numbers: Carrying Multiple Times

#### Question

What is $89 + 982?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers (** over **, ** over **, ** over **):

$$

\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & & \end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**** Adding the ones, ${\color{blue}9} + {\color{blue}2} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}1}.$ We write ${\color{blue}1}$ below the ** and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the **:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**** Adding the tens, ${\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}8} + {\color{blue}8} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}7}.$ We write ${\color{blue}7}$ below the ** and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the **:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**** Adding the hundreds, ${\color{blue}1} + {\color{blue}0} + {\color{blue}9} = {\color{red}1}{\color{blue}0}.$ We write ${\color{blue}0}$ below the ** and carry ${\color{red}1}$ to the **:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**** Adding the thousands, ${\color{blue}1}+{\color{blue}0}+{\color{blue}0}={\color{blue}1}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \\ & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $89 + 982 = 1,071.$
