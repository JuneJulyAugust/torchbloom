## Lesson

### Introduction

Let's calculate the following difference:

$$

225 - 116

$$

We proceed using the standard algorithm.

The first step is to write the numbers in the usual way, where ones are aligned over ones, tens over tens, and hundreds over hundreds:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**Step 1.** We cannot compute $5 - 6.$ So, we borrow $1$ from the *tens* place. This leaves $2 - 1 = {\color{red}{1}}$ tens and $\color{red}15$ ones:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,15\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**Step 2.** Then, we subtract ones from ones, tens from tens, and hundreds from hundreds:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,15\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $225 - 116 = 109.$

### Example: Subtracting Three-Digit Numbers From Three-Digit Numbers

#### Question

What is $578 - 246?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers (** over **, ** over **, and ** over **):

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**** Subtracting the ones, ${\color{blue}8} - {\color{blue}6} = {\color{blue}2}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**** Subtracting the tens, ${\color{blue}7} - {\color{blue}4} = {\color{blue}3}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**** Subtracting the hundreds, ${\color{blue}5} - {\color{blue}2} = {\color{blue}3}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $578 - 246 = 332.$

### Example: Subtracting Three-Digit Numbers From Three-Digit Numbers: Borrowing Once

#### Question

What is $272 - 190?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**** Subtracting the ones, ${\color{blue}2} - {\color{blue}0} = {\color{blue}2}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**** We cannot compute $7 - 9.$ So, we borrow $1$ from the ** place:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,17\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**** Then, we subtract tens and hundreds:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,17\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\, & \,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,8\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $272 - 190 = 82.$

### Example: Subtracting Three-Digit Numbers From Three-Digit Numbers: Borrowing Twice

#### Question

What is $332 - 174?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**** We cannot compute $2 - 4.$ So, we borrow $1$ from the ** place. Then, subtracting the ones, ${\color{blue}12} - {\color{blue}4} = {\color{blue}8}{:}$

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,12\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,7\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**** We cannot compute $2 - 7.$ So, we borrow $1$ from the ** place. Then, we subtract tens and hundreds:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,12\,\,\, & \,\,\,12\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,7\,\,\, & \,\,\,4\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,5\,\,\, & \,\,\,8\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $332 - 174 = 158.$

### Example: Borrowing From Zero

#### Question

What is $208 - 169?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by subtracting the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

**** We cannot compute $8 - 9,$ and there are $0$ tens to borrow from. So, we borrow $1$ from the ** place first:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,10\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Then, we borrow $1$ from the ** place:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\, & \,\,\,18\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,10\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

**** Then, we subtract ones, tens, and hundreds:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\, & \,\,\,18\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,10\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,2\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,−\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\, & \,\,\,6\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\, & \,\,\,3\,\,\, & \,\,\,9\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $208 - 169 = 39.$
