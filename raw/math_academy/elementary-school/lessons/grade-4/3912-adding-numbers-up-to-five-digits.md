## Lesson

### Introduction

Using the standard algorithm, we can calculate the sum of two numbers containing any number of digits.

Let's use the standard algorithm to compute the following sum:

$$

32,810+8,921

$$

The procedure is the same as the one we've seen before. We start by lining up our numbers:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & & & \\ & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $32,810+8,921 = 41,731.$

### Example: Adding Numbers Up to Five Digits

#### Question

What is $23,861+5,026?$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers:

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,5\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,0\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $23,861+5,026 = 28,887.$

### Example: Adding Numbers Up to Five Digits With Carrying

#### Question

Compute the value of $89,743+32,876.$

#### Explanation

First, we line up our numbers:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$

\begin{aligned} & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & & \\ & & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,8\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,6\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, $89,723+32,876 = 122,619.$

### Example: Adding Numbers Up to Five Digits: Word Problems

#### Question

Mike wrote two essays. There are $11,391$ words in the first essay and $7,942$ words in the second essay. How many words did Mike write altogether?

#### Explanation

To find how many words Mike wrote altogether, we need to calculate the value of

$$

11,391 + 7,942.

$$

First, we line up our numbers:

$$

\begin{aligned} & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

Next, we proceed by adding the numbers in each place value (from right to left):

$$

\begin{aligned} & & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & & \\ & & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, \\ \,\,\,\,+\,\,\,\, & & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,7\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,4\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,2\,\,\,\, \\ & \,\,\,\,\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,1\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,9\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\, & \,\,\,\,3\,\,\,\,\end{aligned}

$$

So, $11,391 + 7,942 = 19,333.$

Therefore, Mike wrote $19,333$ words in total.
