### Introduction

In some real-world situations, the same change recurs. When the same amount is added repeatedly, we call this **repeated addition**.

Repeated addition can also be written using multiplication.

For example, suppose a liquid has a temperature of $-6^\circ\text{C}.$ The temperature increases by $0.8^\circ\text{C}$ every minute for $4$ minutes.

We can represent the temperature change as repeated addition:

$$



4



$$

Because the same number is added several times, we can write the repeated addition using multiplication:

$$



0.8 + 0.8 + 0.8 + 0.8 = {4\color{blue}} \times 0.8



$$

So the expression for the temperature after $4$ minutes is

$$



-6 + 4 \times 0.8.



$$

Evaluating the expression, we get

$$



-6 + 4 \times 0.8 = -6 + 3.2 = -2.8.



$$

Therefore, the temperature of the liquid after $4$ minutes is $-2.8\,^\circ\text{C}.$

### Example: Determining an Expression for a Repeated Addition

#### Question

A weather balloon begins at an elevation of $-85\,\textrm{m}$ relative to sea level. It rises $2\dfrac{3}{4}\,\textrm{m}$ every minute for $6$ minutes. What is an expression for the balloon's elevation after $6$ minutes?

#### Explanation

To determine the balloon's elevation after $6$ minutes, we first find the total rise in elevation.

The initial elevation is $-85\,\textrm{m},$ and the balloon rises $2\dfrac{3}{4}\,\textrm{m}$ every minute.

Since this increase happens for $6$ minutes, we can represent the total rise as a repeated addition of $2\dfrac{3}{4}{:}$

$$



6



$$

The repeated addition can also be written using multiplication:

$$



6



$$

Therefore, an expression for the balloon's elevation after $6$ minutes is

$$



-85 + 6 \times 2\dfrac{3}{4}.



$$

### Example: Solving Problems Using a Repeated Addition

#### Question

Lucas has a bank balance of $-58.30.$ He deposits $7.45$ into the account each day for $5$ days. What is Lucas's bank balance after $5$ days?

#### Explanation

To determine the final balance, we first find the total amount deposited.

Lucas deposits $7.45$ each day for $5$ days. This can be written as repeated addition:

$$



-58.30 \;\underbrace{+\;7.45+7.45+7.45+7.45+7.45}_{\text{\color{blue}5 times}}



$$

The repeated addition can also be written using multiplication:

$$



\underbrace{7.45+7.45+7.45+7.45+7.45}_{\text{\color{blue}5 times}} = 5 \times 7.45



$$

So the expression becomes

$$



-58.30 + 5 \times 7.45



$$

Evaluating the multiplication first, we have

$$



5 \times 7.45 = 37.25



$$

Then, we evaluate the addition:

$$



-58.30 + 37.25 = -21.05



$$

Therefore, Lucas's bank balance after $5$ days is $-21.05.$

### Repeated Subtraction

Sometimes the same decrease happens again and again. When the same amount is subtracted repeatedly, we call this **repeated subtraction**.

Repeated subtraction can also be written using multiplication. In particular, subtracting the same number several times is equivalent to multiplying by a negative number.

For example, suppose Marcus has a bank balance of $84.50.$ He withdraws $7.25$ from the account each day for $4$ days.

To determine an expression for the final balance, we first find the total amount withdrawn.

Marcus withdraws $7.25$ each day for $4$ days. This can be written as a repeated subtraction:

$$



84.50 \;\underbrace{-\;7.25-7.25-7.25-7.25}_{\text{\color{blue}4 times}}



$$

The repeated subtraction can also be written using multiplication:

$$



\underbrace{-7.25-7.25-7.25-7.25}_{\text{\color{blue}4 times}} = 4 \times (-7.25)



$$

So the expression for Marcus's bank balance after $4$ days is

$$



84.50 + 4 \times (-7.25).



$$

### Example: Determining an Expression for a Repeated Subtraction

#### Question

A diving robot begins at an elevation of $-48\,\textrm{m}$ relative to sea level. It descends $2\dfrac{3}{4}\,\textrm{m}$ every minute for $5$ minutes. What is an expression for the robot's elevation after $5$ minutes?

#### Explanation

To determine the robot's elevation after $5$ minutes, we first find the total decrease in elevation.

The initial elevation is $-48\,\textrm{m},$ and the robot descends $2\dfrac{3}{4}\,\textrm{m}$ every minute.

Since this decrease happens for $5$ minutes, we can represent the total decrease as a repeated subtraction of $2\dfrac{3}{4}{:}$

$$



5



$$

The repeated subtraction can also be written using multiplication:

$$



5



$$

Therefore, an expression for the robot's elevation after $5$ minutes is

$$



-48 + 5 \times \left(-2\dfrac{3}{4}\right).



$$

### Example: Solving Problems Using a Repeated Subtraction

#### Question

A container of liquid has a temperature of $18.2^\circ\text{C}.$ The temperature decreases by $2.7^\circ\text{C}$ every minute for $4$ minutes. What is the temperature of the liquid after $4$ minutes?

#### Explanation

To determine the final temperature, we find the total decrease in temperature.

The temperature decreases by $2.7^\circ\text{C}$ each minute for $4$ minutes. This can be written as a repeated subtraction:

$$



4



$$

The repeated subtraction can also be written using multiplication:

$$



4



$$

So the expression becomes

$$



18.2 + 4 \times (-2.7)



$$

Evaluating the multiplication first, we have

$$



4 \times (-2.7) = -10.8.



$$

Then, we evaluate the addition:

$$



18.2 + (-10.8) = 7.4



$$

Therefore, the temperature after $4$ minutes is $7.4^\circ\text{C}.$
