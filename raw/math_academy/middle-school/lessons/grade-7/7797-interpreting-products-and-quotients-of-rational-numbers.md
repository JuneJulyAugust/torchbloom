## Lesson

### Introduction

In this lesson, we will learn how to interpret products and quotients of rational numbers in real-world situations. This will help us understand what an expression means in context, not just how to calculate it.

For example, suppose an elevation change is $-3.2$ feet each minute for $5$ minutes. To interpret the product

$$



(-3.2) \cdot 5,



$$

we think about what each number means in context.

In our case,

- $-3.2$ means the elevation changes by $-3.2$ feet each minute, and

- $5$ means this happens for $5$ minutes.

When the same change happens again and again for several minutes, multiplying gives the total change.

Therefore, the product $(-3.2)\cdot 5$ represents a total change in elevation over $5$ minutes.

Let's see another example.

### Example: Interpreting Products of Rational Numbers in Context

#### Question

A bank account decreases by $4.5$ dollars each day for $6$ days. Interpret the product $(-4.5)\cdot 6$ in context.

#### Explanation

Recall that a decrease is represented by a negative number. So, a decrease of $4.5$ dollars each day is represented by the negative number $-4.5.$

In our case,

- $-4.5$ means the bank account changes by $-4.5$ dollars each day, and

- $6$ means this happens for $6$ days.

Therefore, the expression $(-4.5)\cdot 6$ represents the following:

A total decrease in the bank account over $6$ days.

### Interpreting Quotients

Now, suppose a bank account changes by $-24.6$ dollars over $3$ days.

To interpret the quotient

$$



(-24.6)\div 3,



$$

we think about what each number means in context.

In our case,

- $-24.6$ represents the total change in the bank account in dollars, and

- $3$ represents the number of days over which this change happens.

When a total change is shared equally across several days, dividing gives the change for each day.

Therefore, the expression $(-24.6)\div 3$ represents the change in the bank account each day.

### Example: Interpreting Quotients of Rational Numbers in Context

#### Question

A temperature decreased by $6.6$ degrees through $2$ equal drops. Interpret the quotient $(-6.6) \div 2$ in context.

#### Explanation

Recall that a decrease is represented by a negative number. So, a decrease by $6.6$ degrees is represented by the negative number $-6.6.$

In our case,

- $-6.6$ means the temperature changes by $-6.6$ degrees, and

- $2$ means this change happens through $2$ equal drops.

Therefore, the expression $(-6.6) \div 2$ represents the following:

The change in temperature for each drop.

### Choosing Between Products and Quotients in Context

Notice that:

- When a situation gives a change for each unit and asks for the total change, we use multiplication.

- When a situation gives a total change and asks for the change for each unit, we use division.

For example:

- If an elevation changes by $-3.2$ feet each minute for $5$ minutes, then $(-3.2)\cdot 5$ represents the total change.

- If a bank account changes by $-24.6$ dollars over $3$ days, then $(-24.6) \div 3$ represents the change each day.

So, to choose the correct operation, we ask the following:

Are we finding a total change, or are we finding the change for each unit?

### Example: Choosing Products and Quotients in Rational-Number Contexts

#### Question

A town's population decreases by $2.3$ thousand people each year for $4$ years. Which expression represents the total change in population?

#### Explanation

Recall that a decrease is represented by a negative number. So, decreasing by $2.3$ thousand people each year is represented by the negative number $-2.3.$

Since this happens for $4$ years, we multiply the change each year by the number of years. Therefore, the expression is

$$



(-2.3) \cdot 4.



$$
