### Introduction

If you deposit money in a bank, it is common for the bank to pay you **interest** on your money. The interest is just a small amount that the bank pays you, in exchange for your lending them your money.

In this lesson, we will focus on the case of **simple interest**. With simple interest, the interest amount depends only on

- the amount of money you deposited in the bank, called the **principal**,

- the duration of *time* for which you keep the principal in the bank, and

- the *rate* at which the interest accrues.

Simple interest is computed according to the formula

$$



\textrm{Simple Interest} = \textrm{Principal} \times \textrm{Rate} \times \textrm{Time}.



$$

Let's look at an example using the simple interest formula.

### An Example of Computing the Simple Interest

Suppose that Anna puts $200$ in the bank at a $3\%$ simple interest rate, for $4$ years. How much interest will she earn, and what will be her final balance?

First, we convert the rate to a decimal:

$$



3\% = 3 \div 100 = 0.03



$$

Now, using the formula, we get

$$



\begin{aligned}Simple Interest & =Principal×Rate×Time \\ & =200×0.03×4 \\ & =24.\end{aligned}



$$

So, after $4$ years, Anna will have earned $24$ in interest. To find the final balance, we add the interest amount to the principal:

$$



200 + 24 = 224



$$

So, Anna will have a balance of $224$ after $4$ years.

### Example: Calculating Interest Earned

#### Question

A sum of $500$ is deposited in the bank. It earns simple interest of $8\%$ per year. How much interest is earned after $6$ years?

#### Explanation

Simple interest is computed according to the formula

$$



\textrm{Simple Interest} = \textrm{Principal} \times \textrm{Rate} \times \textrm{Time}.



$$

Here, the principal is $500,$ the rate is $8\%$ per year, and the time is $6$ years. Converting the rate to a decimal, we have

$$



8\% = 8 \div 100 = 0.08 \, .



$$

Now, using the formula, we get

$$



\begin{aligned}Simple Interest & =Principal×Rate×Time \\ & =500×0.08×6 \\ & =240.\end{aligned}



$$

So, the amount of interest earned is $240.$

### Example: Calculating the Final Balance

#### Question

A sum of $1,000$ is deposited in the bank. It earns simple interest of $5\%$ per year. What is the final balance after $3$ years?

#### Explanation

The principal is $1,000,$ the rate is $5\%$ per year, and the time is $3$ years.

Converting the rate to a decimal, we have

$$



5\% = 5 \div 100 = 0.05 \, .



$$

Now, using the formula for simple interest, we get

$$



\begin{aligned}Simple Interest & =Principal×Rate×Time \\ & =1,000×0.05×3 \\ & =150.\end{aligned}



$$

So, the amount of interest earned is $150.$ To find the final balance, we add the interest amount to the principal:

$$



1,000 + 150 = 1,150



$$

So, the final balance will be $1,150.$

### Solving for Missing Variables

Now, suppose that Daniel deposited $8,000$ in his bank account. It earns simple interest at a rate of $0.05$ per year. After some years, the final balance was $10,000.$

How long did Daniel hold the deposit in the bank?

First, let's identify the given information:

- Principal: $8,000$

- Rate: $0.05$ per year

- Final Balance: $10,000$

Now, we can find the interest earned. To do that, we subtract the principal from the final balance:

$$



10,000 - 8,000 = 2,000



$$

So, the amount of interest earned is $2,000.$

Next, we use the interest formula:

$$



\textrm{Simple Interest} = \textrm{Principal} \times \textrm{Rate} \times \textrm{Time}



$$

Let $n$ be the number of years the money was in the bank. Then we write:

$$



\begin{aligned}2,000 & =8,000×0.05×𝑛 \\ 2,000 & =400𝑛 \\ 400𝑛 & =2,000 \\ \frac{400𝑛}{400} & =\frac{2,000}{400} \\ 𝑛 & =5\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, the deposit was held in the bank for $5$ years.

### Example: Calculating the Duration of Time

#### Question

A sum of $300$ is deposited in the bank. It earns simple interest of $2\%$ per year. After some number of years, the final balance is $330.$ How long was the deposit kept in the bank?

#### Explanation

The principal is $300,$ and the interest rate is $2\%$ per year. Converting the interest rate to a decimal, we get

$$



2\% = 2 \div 100 = 0.02 \, .



$$

To find the interest amount, we subtract the principal from the final balance:

$$



330 - 300 = 30



$$

So the interest amount is $30.$

Let $n$ be the number of years for which the deposit was kept in the bank. Using the formula, then, we have:

$$



\begin{aligned}Simple Interest & =Principal×Rate×Time \\ 30 & =300×0.02×𝑛 \\ 30 & =6𝑛 \\ 𝑛 & =\frac{30}{6} \\ 𝑛 & =5\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, the deposit was kept in the bank for $5$ years.

### Example: Calculating the Interest Rate

#### Question

A sum of $800$ is deposited in the bank. It earns simple interest and after $4$ years, the final balance is $992.$ What is the interest rate?

#### Explanation

The principal is $800,$ and the time is $4$ years. To find the interest amount, we subtract the principal from the final balance:

$$



992 - 800 = 192



$$

So the interest amount is $192.$

Let $r$ be the interest rate. Using the formula, then, we have:

$$



\begin{aligned}Simple Interest & =Principal×Rate×Time \\ 192 & =800×𝑟×4 \\ 192 & =3,200𝑟 \\ 𝑟 & =\frac{192}{3,200} \\ 𝑟 & =0.06\end{aligned}



$$

Finally, converting the rate $r=0.06$ to a percent, we get

$$



0.06 = 0.06 \times 100\% = 6 \%.



$$

Therefore, the interest rate is $6\%.$
