## Lesson

### Introduction

To convert a percentage to a decimal, we must divide the number in front of the percentage sign by $100.$

To demonstrate, let's convert the following percentage to a decimal:

$$



15 \%



$$

We need to divide $15$ by $100.$ To divide a number by $100,$ we move the decimal point two places to the left:

$$



15 \div 100 = 0.15



$$

Therefore, we conclude that $15 \%$ expressed as a decimal is $0.15.$

### Example: Converting Percentages to Decimals

#### Question

What is $8.7 \%$ as a decimal?

#### Explanation

To convert $8.7 \%$ into a decimal, we divide $8.7$ by $100.$

Remember that to divide a number by $100,$ we move the decimal point two places to the left:

$$



8.7 \div 100 = 0.087



$$

So, $8.7 \%$ is the same as $0.087.$

### Converting Decimals to Percentages

To convert a decimal into a percentage, we need to multiply the decimal by $100.$

To demonstrate, let's convert the following decimal to a percentage:

$$



0.65



$$

We need to multiply $0.65$ by $100.$ Now, to multiply a number by $100,$ we move the decimal point two places to the right:

$$



0.65 \times 100 = 65



$$

Finally, we write the result using the percent sign. So, $0.65$ is the same as $65\%.$

### Example: Converting Decimals to Percentages

Remember that to multiply a number by $100,$ we move the decimal point two places to the right:

$$



0.7 \times 100 = 70



$$

Finally, we write the result using the percent sign. So, our final answer is $70 \%.$
