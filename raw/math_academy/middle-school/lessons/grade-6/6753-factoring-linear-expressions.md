### Introduction

When we **factor** a linear expression, we rewrite it as a product.

A common situation is when two terms share a greatest common factor (GCF). To factor, we do two steps:

1. Write each term as a product using the GCF.

2. Pull the GCF out in front of parentheses.

To demonstrate, let's factor the expression $32x + 12.$
