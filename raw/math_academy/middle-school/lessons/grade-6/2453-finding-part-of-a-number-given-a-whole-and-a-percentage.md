## Lesson

### Introduction

We can find a percentage part of a number using the following formula:

$$



\text{part} = \text{percentage} \times \text{whole}



$$

To illustrate, let's calculate $16 \%$ of $300.$

Here, the whole is $300$ and the percentage is $16 \%.$ So, the part is given by

$$



\text{part} = 16\% \times 300.



$$

But how do we multiply a percentage by a number? We have two methods of doing this. Let's apply both and check that they give the same answer.

**Method 1**

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal:

$$



16\% = 16 \div 100 = 0.16



$$

Then, we apply our formula as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}part & =percentage×whole \\ & =16\%×300 \\ & =0.16×300 \\ & =48\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, $16\%$ of $300$ is $48.$

**Method 2**

First, we convert the percentage to a fraction:

$$



16\% = \dfrac{16}{100}



$$

Then, we apply our formula as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}part & =percentage×whole \\ & =16\%×300 \\ & =\frac{16}{100}×300 \\ & =16×\frac{300}{100} \\ & =16×3 \\ & =48\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, $16 \%$ of $300$ is $48.$

### Example: Finding Part of a Number Given a Whole and a Percentage

#### Question

What is $4 \%$ of $35?$

#### Explanation

To solve this problem, we can use the following formula:

$$



\text{part}= \text{percentage}\times \text{whole}



$$

In our case, the whole is $35$, and the percentage is $4 \%.$

****

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal:

$$



4 \% = 4 \div 100 = 0.04



$$

Then, we apply our formula as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}part & =percentage×whole \\ & =4\%×35 \\ & =0.04×35 \\ & =1.4\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, $4 \%$ of $35$ is $1.4.$

****

First, we convert the percentage to a fraction:

$$



4 \% = \dfrac{4}{100}



$$

Then, we apply our formula as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}part & =percentage×whole \\ & =4\%×35 \\ & =\frac{4}{100}×35 \\ & =\frac{140}{100} \\ & =1.4\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, $4 \%$ of $35$ is $1.4.$

### Example: Finding Part of a Number Given a Whole and a Percentage Greater Than 100%

#### Question

What is $140 \%$ of $30?$

#### Explanation

To solve this problem, we can use the following formula:

$$



\text{part}= \text{percentage}\times \text{whole}



$$

In our case, the whole is $30$, and the percentage is $140\%.$

****

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal:

$$



140 \% = 140 \div 100 = 1.4



$$

Then, we apply our formula as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}part & =percentage×whole \\ & =140\%×30 \\ & =1.4×30 \\ & =42\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, $140 \%$ of $30$ is $42.$

****

First, we convert the percentage to a fraction:

$$



140 \% = \dfrac{140}{100} = \dfrac{140 \div 20}{100\div 20} = \dfrac{7}{5}



$$

Then, we apply our formula as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}part & =percentage×whole \\ & =140\%×30 \\ & =\frac{7}{5}×30 \\ & =7×\frac{30}{5} \\ & =7×6 \\ & =42\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, $140 \%$ of $30$ is $42.$

### Example: Finding Part of a Number Given a Whole and a Decimal Percentage

#### Question

What is $3.6 \%$ of $25?$

#### Explanation

To solve this problem, we can use the following formula:

$$



\text{part}= \text{percentage}\times \text{whole}



$$

In our case, the whole is $25$, and the percentage is $3.6 \%.$

****

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal:

$$



3.6 \% = 3.6 \div 100 = 0.036



$$

Then, we apply our formula as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}part & =percentage×whole \\ & =3.6\%×25 \\ & =0.036×25 \\ & =0.9\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, $3.6 \%$ of $25$ is $0.9.$

****

First, we convert the percentage to a fraction:

$$



3.6 \% = \dfrac{3.6}{100} = \dfrac{3.6 \times 10}{100 \times 10} = \dfrac{36}{1,000}



$$

Then, we apply our formula as follows:

$$



\begin{aligned}part & =percentage×whole \\ & =3.6\%×25 \\ & =\frac{36}{1,000}×25 \\ & =\frac{900}{1,000} \\ & =0.9\end{aligned}



$$

Therefore, $3.6 \%$ of $25$ is $0.9.$
