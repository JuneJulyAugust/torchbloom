## Lesson

### Introduction

A **one-step multiplication equation** is an equation like

$$



6x=15,



$$

where the variable is multiplied by a number.

To solve a one-step multiplication equation, we isolate the variable by reversing the operation that is applied to it.

For example, in the equation above, the variable $x$ is *multiplied* by $6.$ The opposite of multiplying by $6$ is *dividing* by $6.$

So, we divide both sides of the equation by $6$ and simplify:

$$



\begin{aligned}6𝑥 & =15 \\ 6𝑥÷6 & =15÷6 \\ (6÷6)𝑥 & =\frac{15}{6} \\ 1𝑥 & =\frac{15÷3}{6÷3} \\ 𝑥 & =\frac{5}{2}\end{aligned}



$$

This isolates the variable, so we have found the solution. Notice that the solution is a fraction. When solving multiplication equations, the answer may be a fraction, even if the initial equation only contains whole numbers.

**Note**: Dividing by the coefficient indeed removes it from the left-hand side. This is because dividing a product by a number is equivalent to dividing its coefficient:

$$



6x \div 6 = (6 \div 6)x



$$

Recall that if the variable appears on the right-hand side (e.g., $15=6x$), you may first swap the left and right sides of the equation to get $6x=15.$

### One-Step Multiplication Equations With Rational Coefficients

Sometimes, the coefficient of the variable in a multiplication equation is a fraction, such as in the equation

$$



\dfrac{4}{5}x=2.



$$

To isolate $x,$ we need to undo multiplication by $\dfrac{4}{5}.$ We could do this by dividing both sides by $\dfrac{4}{5}$ as before.

However, dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its **reciprocal**. So, to solve a multiplication equation with a rational coefficient, we multiply both sides by the reciprocal of the coefficient.

In our case, the reciprocal of $\dfrac{4}{5}$ is $\dfrac{5}{4}.$ We multiply both sides of the equation by $\dfrac{5}{4}$ and simplify.

On the left-hand side, we can re-group the numbers to multiply the fractions first. On the right-hand side, we can multiply fractions by first swapping denominators:

$$



\begin{aligned}\frac{4}{5}𝑥 & =2 \\ \frac{4}{5}𝑥×\frac{5}{4} & =2×\frac{5}{4} \\ (\frac{4}{5}×\frac{5}{4})𝑥 & =\frac{2}{4}×\frac{5}{1} \\ 1𝑥 & =\frac{1}{2}×\frac{5}{1} \\ 𝑥 & =\frac{5}{2}\end{aligned}



$$

This isolates the variable, so we have found the solution.

### Example: Solving Multiplication Equations With Rational Coefficients

#### Question

If $\dfrac{4}{9}a=6,$ then $a=$

#### Explanation

To solve a linear equation, we isolate the variable by reversing the operations applied to it, performing each inverse operation on both sides of the equation step by step.

The opposite operation to multiplying by a fraction is multiplying by its reciprocal.

In this case, the variable $a$ is ** by $\dfrac{4}{9}.$ To isolate $a,$ we can perform the ** operation, which is ** by $\dfrac{9}{4}.$ So, we multiply both sides of the equation by $\dfrac{9}{4}{:}$

$$



\begin{aligned}\frac{4}{9}𝑎 & =6 \\ \frac{4}{9}𝑎×\frac{9}{4} & =6×\frac{9}{4} \\ (\frac{4}{9}×\frac{9}{4})𝑎 & =\frac{6}{4}×\frac{9}{1} \\ 1𝑎 & =\frac{3}{2}×\frac{9}{1} \\ 𝑎 & =\frac{27}{2}\end{aligned}



$$
