## Lesson

### Introduction

Recall that to solve a linear equation, we isolate the variable by reversing the operations applied to it.

So if a variable is multiplied by a number, we use the opposite operation, which is division.

Whatever operation we perform on one side of the equation, we must perform the same operation on the other side to keep the equation balanced.

For example, consider the equation

$$



6x = 42.



$$

Since $x$ is multiplied by $6,$ we divide both sides by $6$:

$$



\begin{aligned}6𝑥 & =42 \\ 6𝑥÷6 & =42÷6\end{aligned}



$$

Now simplify each side:

$$



\begin{aligned}(6÷6)𝑥 & =42÷6 \\ 1𝑥 & =7 \\ 𝑥 & =7.\end{aligned}



$$

**Note**: If the variable appears on the right-hand side (e.g., $15=3y$), you may first swap the left and right sides of the equation to get $3y=15.$

### Example: Solving Multiplication Equations

#### Question

Given that $4m = 28,$ determine the value of $m.$

#### Explanation

To solve a linear equation, we isolate the variable by reversing the operations applied to it, performing each inverse operation on both sides of the equation step by step.

In this case, the variable $m$ is ** by $4.$ To isolate $m,$ we perform the ** operation, which is ** by $4.$ So, we divide both sides of the equation by $4{:}$

$$



\begin{aligned}4𝑚 & =28 \\ 4𝑚÷4 & =28÷4 \\ (4÷4)𝑚 & =7 \\ 1𝑚 & =7 \\ 𝑚 & =7\end{aligned}



$$

### Example: Solving Equations With the Variable on the Right-Hand Side

#### Question

Given that $45 = 9x,$ determine the value of $x.$

#### Explanation

To solve a linear equation, we isolate the variable by reversing the operations applied to it, performing each inverse operation on both sides of the equation step by step.

First, let's swap the left and right-hand sides so that the variable is on the left-hand side.

$$



9x = 45



$$

In this case, the variable $x$ is ** by $9.$ To isolate $x,$ we perform the ** operation, which is ** by $9.$ So, we divide both sides of the equation by $9{:}$

$$



\begin{aligned}9𝑥 & =45 \\ 9𝑥÷9 & =45÷9 \\ (9÷9)𝑥 & =5 \\ 1𝑥 & =5 \\ 𝑥 & =5\end{aligned}



$$
