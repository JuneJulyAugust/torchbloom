## Lesson

### Introduction

The method of subtracting numbers in scientific notation with different powers of ten is similar to adding them. We follow the usual three steps:

- **Step 1**: Identify the smaller number.

- **Step 2**: Rewrite the smaller number to have the same power of ten as the larger number.

- **Step 3**: Subtract the two numbers as usual.

For instance, let's compute the following difference:

$$

\left(9 \times 10^{7} \right) - \left(8 \times 10^{6} \right)

$$

The second number is the smaller of the two, so we rewrite it to have the same power of $10$ as the first number, as follows:

$$

\begin{aligned}8×10^{6} & = \\ 8×10^{7−1} & = \\ 8×10^{−1+7} & = \\ 8×10^{−1}×10^{7} & = \\ 0.8×10^{7} & \end{aligned}

$$

Finally, we can carry out the subtraction as usual:

$$

\begin{aligned}(9×10^{7})−(8×10^{6}) & = \\ (9×10^{7})−(0.8×10^{7}) & = \\ (9−0.8)×10^{7} & = \\ 8.2×10^{7} & \end{aligned}

$$

### Example: Subtracting Numbers With Different Positive Powers of Ten

#### Question

Express $\left(6.2 \times 10^{13} \right) - \left(8.1 \times 10^{12} \right)$ in scientific notation.

#### Explanation

The second number is the smaller of the two, so we rewrite it to have the same power of $10$ as the first number, as follows:

$$

\begin{aligned}8.1×10^{12} & = \\ 8.1×10^{13−1} & = \\ 8.1×10^{−1+13} & = \\ 8.1×10^{−1}×10^{13} & = \\ 0.81×10^{13} & \end{aligned}

$$

Now, carrying out the subtraction, we get

$$

\begin{aligned}(6.2×10^{13})−(8.1×10^{12}) & = \\ (6.2×10^{13})−(0.81×10^{13}) & = \\ (6.2−0.81)×10^{13} & = \\ 5.39\,×10^{13}. & \end{aligned}

$$

### Example: Subtracting Numbers With Different Negative Powers of Ten

#### Question

Express $\left(3 \times 10^{-9} \right) - \left(6 \times 10^{-10} \right)$ in scientific notation.

#### Explanation

The second number is the smaller of the two, so we rewrite it to have the same power of $10$ as the first number, as follows:

$$

\begin{aligned}6×10^{−10} & = \\ 6×10^{−9−1} & = \\ 6×10^{−1−9} & = \\ 6×10^{−1}×10^{−9} & = \\ 0.6×10^{−9} & \end{aligned}

$$

Now, carrying out the subtraction, we get

$$

\begin{aligned}(3×10^{−9})−(6×10^{−10}) & = \\ (3×10^{−9})−(0.6×10^{−9}) & = \\ (3−0.6)×10^{−9} & = \\ 2.4\,×10^{−9}. & \end{aligned}

$$

### Example: Subtracting Numbers Where the Powers Differ by More Than One

#### Question

Express $\left(4 \times 10^{5} \right) - \left(8 \times 10^{3}\right)$ in scientific notation.

#### Explanation

The second number is the smaller of the two, so we rewrite it to have the same power of $10$ as the first number, as follows:

$$

\begin{aligned}8×10^{3} & = \\ 8×10^{5−2} & = \\ 8×10^{−2+5} & = \\ 8×10^{−2}×10^{5} & = \\ 0.08×10^{5} & \end{aligned}

$$

Now, carrying out the subtraction, we get

$$

\begin{aligned}(4×10^{5})−(8×10^{3}) & = \\ (4×10^{5})−(0.08×10^{5}) & = \\ (4−0.08)×10^{5} & = \\ 3.92\,×10^{5}. & \end{aligned}

$$

### Example: Word Problems

#### Question

The flow rate of the Amazon River is $2.8 \times 10^{8}$ liters per second, while the flow rate of the Orinoco River is $3.3 \times 10^{7}$ liters per second. How much greater is the flow in the Amazon river compared to the Orinoco river?

#### Explanation

We need to write the following expression in scientific notation:

$$

2.8 \times 10^{8} - 3.3 \times 10^{7}.

$$

The second number is the smaller of the two, so we rewrite it to have the same power of $10$ as the first number, as follows:

$$

\begin{aligned}3.3×10^{7} & = \\ 3.3×10^{8−1} & = \\ 3.3×10^{−1+8} & = \\ 3.3×10^{−1}×10^{8} & = \\ 0.33×10^{8} & \end{aligned}

$$

Now, carrying out the subtraction, we get

$$

\begin{aligned}(2.8×10^{8})−(3.3×10^{7}) & \\ (2.8×10^{8})−(0.33×10^{8}) & \\ (2.8−0.33)×10^{8} & \\ 2.47×10^{8}. & \end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, the Amazon river flow is $2.47 \times 10^{8}$ liters per second more than the Orinoco.
