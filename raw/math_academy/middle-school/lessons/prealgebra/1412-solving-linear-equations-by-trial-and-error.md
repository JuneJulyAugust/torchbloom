### Introduction

An **equation** consists of two mathematical expressions separated by an equals $(=)$ sign. The expressions may or may not contain variables. Two examples of equations are shown below:

$$

2 + 3 = 5 \qquad\qquad 2 + x = 5

$$

An equation is **true** if the expression to the left side of the equals sign is equal to the expression on the right side. Otherwise, if the left side is not equal to the right side, then the equation is **false**. For instance,

- the equation $2 + 3 = 5$ is **true**, whereas

- the equation $2+2 = 5$ is **false**.

As a result, the equation

$$

2 + x = 5

$$

is true *only if* $x=3.$ We say that $x=3$ is the **solution** to the equation $2+x=5.$

### Example: Solving Linear Equations Involving Addition

#### Question

If $z + 9 = 12,$ then what is the value of $z?$

#### Explanation

If we substitute ${\color{blue}{z}}={\color{blue}{3}}$ into the given equation, we get a true statement:

$$

\begin{aligned}𝑧+9 & =12 \\ 3+9 & =12 \\ 12 & =12\,✓\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, the solution is $z=3.$

### Example: Solving Linear Equations Involving Subtraction

#### Question

What is the solution to the equation $a - 3 = 4?$

#### Explanation

If we substitute ${\color{blue}{a}}={\color{blue}{7}}$ into the given equation, we get a true statement:

$$

\begin{aligned}𝑎−3 & =4 \\ 7−3 & =4 \\ 4 & =4\,✓\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, the solution is $a=7.$

### Example: Solving Linear Equations Involving Multiplication

#### Question

If $2b = 8,$ then $b=$

#### Explanation

If we substitute ${\color{blue}{b}}={\color{blue}{4}}$ into the given equation, we get a true statement:

$$

\begin{aligned}2𝑏 & =8 \\ 2⋅4 & =8 \\ 8 & =8\,✓\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, the solution is $b=4.$

### Example: Solving Linear Equations Involving Division

#### Question

If $\dfrac{15}{c} = 5,$ then $c=$

#### Explanation

If we substitute ${\color{blue}{c}}={\color{blue}{3}}$ into the given equation, we get a true statement:

$$

\begin{aligned}\frac{15}{𝑐} & =5 \\ \frac{15}{3} & =5 \\ 5 & =5\,✓\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, the solution is $c = \boxed{\color{blue}3}.$
