## Lesson

### Introduction

Dividing with negative numbers follows similar rules as multiplying with negative numbers.

First of all, a negative number divided by a positive number gives a negative number. We can picture this rule as

$$

\begin{aligned}(−)\,÷\,(+)\,=\,(−).\end{aligned}

$$

For example, let's use this rule to find the value of $(-6) \div 2.$

Here, since we are dividing a negative number $\color{red}(-6)$ by a positive number ${\color{blue}(2)},$ the result is going to be negative:

$$

3

$$

Now, to compute the number that goes in the box, we divide $6$ by $2$ *without* any signs: $6 \div 2 = 3.$ So, we get

$$

{\color{red}(-6)} \div {\color{blue}2} = {\color{red} -3 }.

$$

### Example: Dividing a Negative Number by a Positive Number

#### Question

Find the value of $-15 \div 3.$

#### Explanation

Here, we are dividing a negative number $\color{red}(-15)$ by a positive one $\color{blue}(3).$ So the result will be negative:

$$

5

$$

Now, to compute the number that goes in the box, we divide $15$ by $3$ without any signs: $15 \div 3 = 5.$ Therefore, we get

$$

{\color{red}(-15)} \div {\color{blue}3} = {\color{red}-5}.

$$

### The Quotient of a Positive and a Negative is a Negative

A positive number divided by a negative number is also a negative number. We can picture this rule as

$$

\begin{aligned}(+)\,÷\,(−)\,=\,(−).\end{aligned}

$$

For instance, to find $8\div (-2),$ we have to divide a positive number $\color{blue}(8)$ by a negative one ${\color{red}(-2)}.$ So the result will be negative:

$$

10

$$

Now, to compute the number that goes in the box, we divide $8$ by $2$ without any signs: $8 \div 2 = 4.$ So, we get

$$

{\color{blue}8} \div {\color{red}(-2)} = {\color{red}-4}.

$$

### Example: Dividing a Positive Number by a Negative Number

#### Question

Calculate the value of $50 \div (-5).$

#### Explanation

We have to divide a positive number $\color{blue}(50)$ by a negative one $\color{red}(-5).$ So the result will be negative:

$$

10

$$

Now, to compute the number that goes in the box, we divide $50$ by $5$ without any signs: $50 \div 5 = 10.$ Therefore, we get

$$

{\color{blue}{50}} \div {\color{red}{(-5)}} = {\color{red}{-10}}.

$$

### The Quotient of a Negative and a Negative is a Positive

Finally, the third rule is that a negative number divided by a negative number is a positive number:

$$

\begin{aligned}(−)\,÷\,(−)\,=\,(+)\end{aligned}

$$

To demonstrate, let's find $-12 \div (-3).$ We have to divide a negative number $\color{red}(-12)$ by another negative number ${\color{red}(-3)}.$ So the result will be positive:

$$

4

$$

Now, to compute the number that goes in the box, we divide $12$ by $3$ without any signs: $12 \div 3 = 4.$ Therefore, we get

$$

{\color{red}(-12)} \div {\color{red}(-3)} = {\color{blue}4}.

$$
