## Lesson

### Introduction

In this lesson, we'll learn to compare numbers expressed in scientific notation.

For example, suppose we want to compare the following two numbers:

$$

7.4 \times 10^{5}, \qquad 6.5 \times 10^{5}

$$

To determine which of them is larger, we note the following:

- Our numbers contain the same power of ten $\left(10^{\color{blue}5}\right).$

- When the powers of ten are the *same*, we *only* need to compare the decimal parts.

Now, since ${\color{red}7.4}$ is *larger than* ${\color{red}6.5},$ i.e.,

$$

{\color{red}7.4} \:\boxed{> }\: {\color{red}6.5}

$$

we can write down the following comparison statement:

$$

\left({\color{red}7.4} \times 10^{\color{blue}5}\right) \:\boxed{> }\: \left( {\color{red}6.5} \times 10^{\color{blue}5} \right)

$$

### Example: Comparing Numbers in Scientific Notation: Same Powers of Ten

#### Question

$$

\left(7.1 \times 10^{-4}\right) \:\boxed{\phantom{A}}\: \left( 8.45 \times 10^{-4} \right)

$$

Which symbols could replace the empty box above to make the statement true?

1. $>$

2. $=$

3. $<$

#### Explanation

Notice that our numbers contain the same power of ten, namely, $10^{\color{blue}-4}.$

To compare numbers in scientific notation with the same power of ten, we compare their decimal parts.

Since ${\color{red}7.1} < {\color{red}8.45},$ we have

$$

\left({\color{red}7.1} \times 10^{\color{blue}-4}\right) \:\boxed{ < }\: \left( {\color{red}8.45} \times 10^{\color{blue}-4} \right).

$$

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."

### Different Powers of Ten

Now, suppose we want to compare the following two numbers:

$$

8.3 \times 10^{-5}, \qquad 6.5 \times 10^{2}

$$

To determine which of them is larger, we note the following:

- Both numbers are positive, and

- they contain *different* powers of ten $\left(10^{\color{blue}-5}\right.$ and $\left.10^{\color{blue}2}\right)\!.$

When positive numbers in scientific notation contain different powers of ten, we can write down a comparison statement by looking at the powers of ten only.

Now, since

$$

{\color{blue}-5} < {\color{blue}2},

$$

we can write down the following comparison statement:

$$

8.3 \times 10^{\color{blue}-5} \:\boxed{ < }\: 6.5 \times 10^{\color{blue}2}

$$

### Example: Comparing Numbers in Scientific Notation: Different Powers of Ten, Positive Exponents

#### Question

$$

\left(2.81 \times 10^{3}\right) \:\boxed{\phantom{A}}\: \left( 2.69 \times 10^{5} \right)

$$

Which symbols could replace the empty box above to make the statement true?

1. $>$

2. $=$

3. $<$

#### Explanation

Notice that our numbers contain different powers of ten:

- $2.81 \times 10^{\color{blue}3}$ contains the $\color{blue}3$rd power of ten

- $2.69 \times 10^{\color{blue}5}$ contains the $\color{blue}5$th power of ten

Since ${\color{blue}3} < {\color{blue}5},$ we have

$$

\left( 2.81 \times 10^{\color{blue}3}\right) \:\boxed{ < }\: \left(2.69 \times 10^{\color{blue}5} \right).

$$

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."

### Example: Comparing Numbers in Scientific Notation: Different Powers of Ten, Negative Exponents

#### Question

$$

\left(7.1 \times 10^{-3}\right) \:\boxed{\phantom{A}}\: \left( 8.7 \times 10^{-4} \right)

$$

Which symbols could replace the empty box above to make the statement true?

1. $>$

2. $=$

3. $<$

#### Explanation

Notice that our numbers contain different powers of ten:

- $7.1 \times 10^{\color{blue}-3}$ contains the ${\color{blue}-3}$rd power of ten

- $8.7 \times 10^{\color{blue}-4}$ contains the ${\color{blue}-4}$th power of ten

Since ${\color{blue}-3} > {\color{blue}-4},$ we have

$$

\left( 7.1 \times 10^{\color{blue}-3}\right) \:\boxed{ > }\: \left( 8.7 \times 10^{\color{blue}-4} \right).

$$

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
