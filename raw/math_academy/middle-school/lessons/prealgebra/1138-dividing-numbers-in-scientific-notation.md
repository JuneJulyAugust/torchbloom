## Lesson

### Introduction

Suppose that we have two numbers in scientific notation, like $4 \times 10^5$ and $2\times 10^3.$ How can we divide one by the other?

To divide $4 \times 10^{5}$ by $2 \times 10^{3},$ we apply the following three steps:

- **Step 1:** Group the decimal parts and the powers of $10$ separately.

- **Step 2:** Divide the decimal parts.

$$

\begin{aligned}\frac{4}{2}×\frac{10^{5}}{10^{3}}=2×\frac{10^{5}}{10^{3}}\end{aligned}

$$

- **Step 3:** Divide the powers of $10$ using the quotient rule for exponents.

$$

\begin{aligned}2×\frac{10^{5}}{10^{3}} & =2×10^{5−3} \\ & =2×10^{2}\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, the final answer is $2\times 10^2.$

### Example: Dividing Numbers With Positive Powers

#### Question

What is $\dfrac{7.2 \times 10^{5}}{1.8 \times 10}$ written in scientific notation?

#### Explanation

To divide two numbers in scientific notation, we separate the coefficients from the powers of $10$ and divide them separately:

$$

\begin{aligned}\frac{7.2×10^{5}}{1.8×10} & =\frac{7.2}{1.8}×\frac{10^{5}}{10^{1}} \\ & =4×10^{5−1} \\ & =4×10^{4}\end{aligned}

$$

### Example: Dividing Numbers With Negative Powers

#### Question

Rounding your answer to three decimal places where appropriate, write $\dfrac{6.32 \times 10^{-4}}{2.4 \times 10^{6}}$ in scientific notation.

#### Explanation

To divide two numbers in scientific notation, we separate the decimal part from the powers of $10$ and divide them separately:

$$

\begin{aligned}\frac{6.32×10^{−4}}{2.4×10^{6}} & =\frac{6.32}{2.4}×\frac{10^{−4}}{10^{6}} \\ & ≈2.633\,3×10^{−4−6} \\ & ≈2.633×10^{−10}\end{aligned}

$$

### Example: Dividing With Negative Numbers

#### Question

Express $\dfrac{8.6 \times 10^{7}}{(-2 \times 10^{5})}$ in scientific notation.

#### Explanation

To divide two numbers in scientific notation, we separate the decimal part from the powers of $10$ and divide them separately. We also remember that a positive number divided by a negative number gives a negative number:

$$

\begin{aligned}\frac{8.6×10^{7}}{(−2×10^{5})} & =−\frac{8.6×10^{7}}{2×10^{5}} \\ & =−\frac{8.6}{2}×\frac{10^{7}}{10^{5}} \\ & =−4.3×10^{7−5} \\ & =−4.3×10^{2}\end{aligned}

$$

### Example: Dividing Numbers and Rewriting the Result

#### Question

What is $\dfrac{2\times 10^{3}}{8 \times 10^{7}}$ written in scientific notation?

#### Explanation

To divide two numbers in scientific notation, we separate the decimal part from the powers of $10$ and divide them separately:

$$

\begin{aligned}\frac{2×10^{3}}{8×10^{7}} & =\frac{2}{8}×\frac{10^{3}}{10^{7}} \\ & =0.25×10^{3−7} \\ & =0.25×10^{−4}\end{aligned}

$$

This is not in scientific notation since $0.25 < 1.$ Writing the decimal part in scientific notation, we get

$$

\begin{aligned}0.25×10^{−4} & =(2.5×10^{−1})×10^{−4} \\ & =2.5×(10^{−1}×10^{−4}) \\ & =2.5×(10^{−1−4}) \\ & =2.5×10^{−5}.\end{aligned}

$$

Therefore, the final answer is $2.5 \times 10^{-5}.$
