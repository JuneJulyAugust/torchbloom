## Lesson

### Introduction

In this lesson, we will learn how to transform linear functions given in tabular form.

For the linear function $f,$ the table shows three values of $x$ and their corresponding values of $f(x).$ If the function $g$ is defined as

$$


g(x) = f(x) - 7,


$$

let's use the given information to find $g.$

Since $f(x)$ is linear, it can be written as

$$


f(x) = mx + b.


$$

First, we find the slope $m$ using the points $(x_1, y_1)=(-2,10)$ and $(x_2, y_2)=(2,2){:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑚 & =\frac{𝑦_{2}−𝑦_{1}}{𝑥_{2}−𝑥_{1}} \\ & =\frac{2−10}{2−(−2)} \\ & =\frac{−8}{4} \\ & =−2\end{aligned}


$$

Next, we find the $y$-intercept $b$ using the point $(-2, 10){:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑓(𝑥) & =−2⋅𝑥+𝑏 \\ 10 & =−2(−2)+𝑏 \\ 𝑏 & =6\end{aligned}


$$

Thus, we have

$$


f(x) = -2x + 6,


$$

from which it follows that

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑔(𝑥) & =𝑓(𝑥)−7 \\ & =(−2𝑥+6)−7 \\ & =−2𝑥−1.\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, $g(x) = -2x - 1.$

### Example: Translating Linear Functions Given in Tabular Form

#### Question

The table above shows three $x$-values and their corresponding $y$-values that lie on the line $u$ in the $xy$-plane. Line $v$ is the result of translating line $u$ up $8$ units in the $xy$-plane. Which of the following equations represents the line $v?$

#### Explanation

Since the relationship is linear, it can be written as

$$


y = mx + c.


$$

First, we find the slope $m$ using the points $(x_1, y_1)=(-4,11)$ and $(x_2, y_2)=(0,3){:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑚 & =\frac{𝑦_{2}−𝑦_{1}}{𝑥_{2}−𝑥_{1}} \\ & =\frac{3−11}{0−(−4)} \\ & =\frac{−8}{4} \\ & =−2\end{aligned}


$$

Next, we find the $y$-intercept $c$ using the point $(0, 3){:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}3 & =(−2)⋅0+𝑐 \\ 𝑐 & =3\end{aligned}


$$

Thus, the equation of the line $u$ is

$$


y = -2x + 3.


$$

Since line $v$ is the result of translating line $u$ by $8$ units up, its equation is

$$


y = (-2x+3) + 8 = -2x+11.


$$

### Example: Identifying a Linear Function Given a Corresponding Nonlinear Function in Tabular Form

#### Question

The table above shows three $x$-values and their corresponding values of $g(x),$ where $g(x)=\dfrac{f(x)}{x+1}$ and $f$ is a linear function. What is the equation of the function $f(x)?$

#### Explanation

First, since $g(x)=\dfrac{f(x)}{x+1},$ we can write $f$ in terms of $g$ by multiplying both sides by $(x+1){:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑔(𝑥) & =\frac{𝑓(𝑥)}{𝑥+1} \\ (𝑥+1)⋅𝑔(𝑥) & =(𝑥+1)⋅\frac{𝑓(𝑥)}{𝑥+1} \\ (𝑥+1)⋅𝑔(𝑥) & =(𝑥+1)⋅\frac{𝑓(𝑥)}{𝑥+1} \\ (𝑥+1)𝑔(𝑥) & =𝑓(𝑥)\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, we have

$$


f(x)=(x+1)g(x).


$$

Substituting in each given value of $x,$ we find coordinates on the line $y=f(x){:}$

- For $x=2,$ we have So, the point $(2,3)$ is on the line.

- For $x=4,$ we have So, the point $(4,10)$ is on the line.

- For $x=8,$ we have So, the point $(8,24)$ is on the line.

So, we now have a table of values for $y = f(x).$

Since $f$ is a linear function, it can be written as

$$


f(x)=mx+c.


$$

Now, we find the slope $m$ using the points $(x_1, y_1)=(2,3)$ and $(x_2, y_2)=(4,10){:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑚 & =\frac{𝑦_{2}−𝑦_{1}}{𝑥_{2}−𝑥_{1}} \\ & =\frac{10−3}{4−2} \\ & =\frac{7}{2}\end{aligned}


$$

Next, we find the $y$-intercept $c$ using the point $(2,3){:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}3 & =\frac{7}{2}(2)+𝑐 \\ 3 & =7+𝑐 \\ 𝑐 & =−4\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, the function is

$$


f(x) = \dfrac{7}{2}x - 4.


$$

### Finding Intercepts

Let's again remind ourselves how to compute the intercepts of linear functions:

- To find the $y$-intercept of a linear relationship $y = f(x),$ we set $x=0$ and solve for $y.$

- To find the $x$-intercept of a linear relationship $y = f(x),$ we set $y=0$ and solve for $x.$

We can apply the same ideas to transformed functions. We just need to remember to find the equation of the transformed function first. Let's see an example.

### Example: Finding Intercepts of Transformed Functions

#### Question

The table above shows three $x$-values and their corresponding $y$-values that lie on the line $k$ in the $xy$-plane. Line $l$ is the result of translating line $k$ down $2$ units in the $xy$-plane. What is the $x$-intercept of the line $l?$

#### Explanation

Since the relationship is linear, it can be written as

$$


y = mx+c.


$$

First, we find the slope $m$ using the points $(x_1, y_1)=(-2, -4)$ and $(x_2, y_2)=(0, -1){:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}𝑚 & =\frac{𝑦_{2}−𝑦_{1}}{𝑥_{2}−𝑥_{1}} \\ & =\frac{−1−(−4)}{0−(−2)} \\ & =\frac{3}{2}.\end{aligned}


$$

Next, we find the $y$-intercept $c$ using the point $(-2, -4){:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}−4 & =\frac{3}{2}⋅(−2)+𝑐 \\ −4 & =−3+𝑐 \\ 𝑐 & =−1.\end{aligned}


$$

Thus, the equation of line $k$ is

$$


y = \dfrac{3}{2}x - 1.


$$

Since line $l$ is the result of translating line $k$ by $2$ units down, its equation is

$$


y = \dfrac{3}{2}x - 3.


$$

Finally, to find the $x$-intercept of line $l,$ we set $y = 0$ in the above equation and solve for $x{:}$

$$


\begin{aligned}0 & =\frac{3}{2}𝑥−3 \\ \frac{3}{2}𝑥 & =3 \\ 𝑥 & =2.\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, the value of $x$-intercept is $2.$
