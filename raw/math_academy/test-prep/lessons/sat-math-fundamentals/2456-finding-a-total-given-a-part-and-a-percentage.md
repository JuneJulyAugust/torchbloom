## Lesson

### Introduction

To find a part given a percent and whole, we can use the following formula:

$$


\textrm{percent} \times \textrm{whole} = \textrm{part}


$$

However, we sometimes want to find a whole given a part and a percent. To do this, we can use the relationship between multiplication and division to rewrite the above formula as follows:

$$


\textrm{whole} = \textrm{part} \div \textrm{percent}


$$

Let's use this formula to find the missing number in the following statement:

$$


0


$$

In this case, the part is $540$, and the percent is $30\%.$

We can express $30\%$ as a fraction as follows:

$$


\dfrac{30}{100} = \dfrac{30\div 10}{100\div 10} = \dfrac{3}{10}


$$

Putting these numbers into our formula for the whole, we get

$$


\begin{aligned}whole & =part÷percent \\ & =540÷30\% \\ & =540÷\frac{3}{10} \\ & =540×\frac{10}{3} \\ & =\frac{540}{3}×10 \\ & =180×10 \\ & =1,800\,.\end{aligned}


$$

So, the missing number is $1,800.$

### Example: Finding a Total Given a Part and a Multiple of Ten Percent

#### Question

If $0$ then what is the missing number?

#### Explanation

We are given that the part is $36,$ the percentage is $50\%,$ and we need to find the whole.

To do this, we can use the following formula:

$$


\textrm{whole} = \textrm{part} \div \textrm{percentage}


$$

Before we can substitute our numbers into the formula, we need to express $50\%$ as a fraction:

$$


\dfrac{50}{100} = \dfrac{50\div 50}{100\div 50} = \dfrac{1}{2}


$$

We can now substitute our numbers into the formula, as follows:

$$


\begin{aligned}whole & =36÷50\% \\ & =36÷\frac{1}{2} \\ & =36×\frac{2}{1} \\ & =36×2 \\ & =72\end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Finding a Total Given a Part and a Percentage

#### Question

$34\%$ of a number is $170.$ What is the number?

#### Explanation

We're given that the part is $170,$ the percentage is $34\%,$ and we need to find the whole.

To do this, we can use the following formula:

$$


\textrm{whole} = \textrm{part} \div \textrm{percentage}


$$

Before we can substitute our numbers into the formula, we need to express $34\%$ as a fraction:

$$


\dfrac{34}{100} = \dfrac{34\div 2}{100\div 2} = \dfrac{17}{50}


$$

We can now substitute our numbers into the formula, as follows:

$$


\begin{aligned}whole & =170÷34\% \\ & =170÷\frac{17}{50} \\ & =170×\frac{50}{17} \\ & =\frac{170}{17}×50 \\ & =10×50 \\ & =500\end{aligned}


$$

### Example: Finding a Total Given a Part and a Percentage: Word Problems

#### Question

In the math club, there are $16$ girls and some boys. If the girls represent $40\%$ of the total members of the club, how many members does the math club have?

#### Explanation

We're given a part and a percentage, and we need to find the whole.

In this case, the part is the number of girls in the math club, which is $16.$ The percentage is $40\%,$ and the whole is the total number of club members.

To find the whole, we can use the following formula:

$$


\textrm{whole} = \textrm{part} \div \textrm{percentage}


$$

Before we can substitute our numbers into the formula, we need to express $40\%$ as a fraction:

$$


\dfrac{40}{100} = \dfrac{40\div 20}{100\div 20} = \dfrac{2}{5}


$$

We can now substitute our numbers into the formula, as follows:

$$


\begin{aligned}whole & =16÷40\% \\ & =16÷\frac{2}{5} \\ & =16×\frac{5}{2} \\ & =\frac{16}{2}×5 \\ & =8×5 \\ & =40\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, the club has $40$ members.
