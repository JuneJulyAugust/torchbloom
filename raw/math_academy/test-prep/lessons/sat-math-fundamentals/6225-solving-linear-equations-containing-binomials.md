## Lesson

### Introduction

In this lesson, we'll learn how to solve linear equations that contain binomials inside parentheses. These types of equations appear frequently in algebra, and being able to simplify them step by step will make solving them straightforward.

For example, let's find the solution to the equation

$$


4x + 3(x + 2) = 34.


$$

First, we expand the expression on the left-hand side and simplify by collecting like terms:

$$


\begin{aligned}4𝑥+3(𝑥+2) & =4𝑥+3𝑥+6 \\ & =7𝑥+6\end{aligned}


$$

So, the equation becomes

$$


\begin{aligned}7𝑥+6 & =34.\end{aligned}


$$

Then, we solve for $x$ using the addition and multiplication principles, as usual:

$$


\begin{aligned}7𝑥+6 & =34 \\ 7𝑥+6−6 & =34−6 \\ 7𝑥 & =28 \\ \frac{7𝑥}{7} & =\frac{28}{7} \\ 𝑥 & =4\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, the solution is $x=4.$

Let's see some more examples.

### Example: Solving Linear Equations Using the Distributive Law

#### Question

What value of $x$ is the solution to the equation $5x + 2(x - 3) = 29?$

#### Explanation

First, we expand the expression on the left-hand side and simplify by collecting like terms:

$$


\begin{aligned}5𝑥+2(𝑥−3) & =5𝑥+2𝑥−6 \\ & =7𝑥−6\end{aligned}


$$

So, the equation becomes

$$


\begin{aligned}7𝑥−6 & =29.\end{aligned}


$$

Next, we solve for $x,$ as usual:

$$


\begin{aligned}7𝑥−6 & =29 \\ 7𝑥−6+6 & =29+6 \\ 7𝑥 & =35 \\ \frac{7𝑥}{7} & =\frac{35}{7} \\ 𝑥 & =5\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, the answer is $x=5.$

### Example: Distributing a Negative Sign

#### Question

Find the solution of the equation $3x - 6(x + 4) = -48.$

#### Explanation

Notice that the coefficient of the binomial on the left-hand side is negative, so we need to be extra careful when expanding this binomial.

First, we expand the expression on the left-hand side and simplify by collecting like terms:

$$


\begin{aligned}3𝑥−6(𝑥+4) & =3𝑥−6𝑥−24 \\ & =−3𝑥−24\end{aligned}


$$

So, the equation becomes

$$


\begin{aligned}−3𝑥−24 & =−48.\end{aligned}


$$

Next, we solve for $x,$ as usual:

$$


\begin{aligned}−3𝑥−24 & =−48 \\ −3𝑥−24+24 & =−48+24 \\ −3𝑥 & =−24 \\ \frac{−3𝑥}{−3} & =\frac{−24}{−3} \\ 𝑥 & =8\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, the answer is $x=8.$

### Example: Solving Equations With Variables on Both Sides

#### Question

What value of $x$ is the solution to the equation $5(2x + 1) - 2 = 9 - 3(2x - 5) - 1?$

#### Explanation

Notice that the coefficient of the binomial on the right-hand side is negative, so we need to be extra careful when expanding this binomial.

We begin by expanding both sides of the equation and simplifying by collecting like terms:

$$


\begin{aligned}5(2𝑥+1)−2 & =9−3(2𝑥−5)−1 \\ 10𝑥+5−2 & =9−6𝑥+15−1 \\ 10𝑥+3 & =23−6𝑥\end{aligned}


$$

Next, we solve the equation for $x,$ as usual:

$$


\begin{aligned}10𝑥+3 & =23−6𝑥 \\ 10𝑥+3−3 & =23−6𝑥−3 \\ 10𝑥 & =20−6𝑥 \\ 10𝑥+6𝑥 & =20−6𝑥+6𝑥 \\ 16𝑥 & =20 \\ \frac{16𝑥}{16} & =\frac{20}{16} \\ 𝑥 & =\frac{5}{4}\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, the solution is $x=\dfrac{5}{4}.$

### Example: Solving Equations With Fractional or Decimals Coefficients

#### Question

What value of $x$ is the solution to the equation $\dfrac{1}{3}x + \dfrac{1}{2}(x + 6) = 7?$

#### Explanation

First, we expand the expression on the left-hand side and simplify by collecting like terms:

$$


\begin{aligned}\frac{1}{3}𝑥+\frac{1}{2}(𝑥+6) & =\frac{1}{3}𝑥+\frac{1}{2}𝑥+3 \\ & =(\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{2})𝑥+3 \\ & =(\frac{2}{6}+\frac{3}{6})𝑥+3 \\ & =\frac{5}{6}𝑥+3\end{aligned}


$$

So, the equation becomes

$$


\begin{aligned}\frac{5}{6}𝑥+3 & =7.\end{aligned}


$$

Next, we multiply both sides by $6$ to get rid of the fractions:

$$


\begin{aligned}6⋅(\frac{5}{6}𝑥+3) & =6⋅(7) \\ 5𝑥+18 & =42\end{aligned}


$$

Finally, we solve for $x,$ as usual:

$$


\begin{aligned}5𝑥+18 & =42 \\ 5𝑥+18−18 & =42−18 \\ 5𝑥 & =24 \\ \frac{5𝑥}{5} & =\frac{24}{5} \\ 𝑥 & =\frac{24}{5}\end{aligned}


$$

Therefore, the answer is $x=\boxed{\dfrac{24}{5}}.$
